7. APPROACH AND METHODOLOGY (BRIEF)
================================================================================

To fulfill the objectives of this assignment under Package No. SD-1 (1799-1970)
and SD-5 (2011-till date), The Legal Empire proposes the following approach:

Phase 1: Inception and Planning
   - Detailed review of Terms of Reference (ToR)
   - Development of a comprehensive work plan and methodology
   - Identification of data sources, repositories, and gazette archives
   - Stakeholder consultation and coordination with the Project Office

Phase 2: Identification and Collection
   - Systematic identification of all delegated/subordinate legislations
     (rules, regulations, by-laws, orders, notifications, etc.) enacted
     during the period 1799-1970 (SD-1) and 2011-till date (SD-5)
   - Collection from official gazettes, government archives, ministry
     records, and other authoritative sources
   - Creation of a master inventory/database of identified legislations

Phase 3: Analysis and Consolidation
   - Legal analysis of each identified subordinate legislation
   - Classification by ministry, subject matter, legal authority, and status
     (active, amended, repealed, or obsolete)
   - Cross-referencing with parent/enabling Acts
   - Identification of inconsistencies, overlaps, and gaps

Phase 4: Codification and Drafting
   - Preparation of the Code of Delegated/Subordinate Legislation
   - Structured compilation with appropriate indexing and referencing
   - Quality review and peer verification

Phase 5: Finalization and Submission
   - Stakeholder review and feedback incorporation
   - Final submission of the Code with supporting documentation
   - Presentation to the Project Office and relevant authorities
