1. OVERVIEW
================================================================================

The Legal Empire proposes a dedicated team of seven (7) qualified key
professionals for Package SD-1, supported by administrative and IT support
staff. The team has been specifically composed to address the scope, complexity,
and historical nature of this assignment covering the period 1799-1970.

The team structure ensures:
   - Strong leadership through a retired Secretary with 35+ years of
     government and legal experience
   - Deep judicial expertise with a retired Senior District Judge and former
     Secretary of Bangladesh Law Commission
   - Specialist capacity for legislative drafting and translation from a
     retired Joint Secretary of LPAD with 25+ years in legislative work
   - Academic research strength from Jagannath University faculty
   - Dedicated data management and IT support capability
   - Independent quality assurance and review at each stage
