1. OVERVIEW
================================================================================

The Legal Empire proposes a dedicated team of seven (7) qualified key
professionals for this assignment, supported by administrative and IT support
staff. The team has been specifically composed to address the scope, complexity,
and dual-package nature of this assignment (SD-1: 1799-1970 and SD-5:
2011-till date).

The team structure ensures:
   - Strong leadership and project management through an experienced Team Leader
   - Specialist capacity for historical (SD-1) and contemporary (SD-5) research
   - Dedicated legislative drafting and codification expertise
   - Digital data management and database development capability
   - Independent quality assurance and review at each stage
