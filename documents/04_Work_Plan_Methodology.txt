1. INTRODUCTION
================================================================================

The Legal Empire is pleased to present this Work Plan and Methodology for the
Preparation of the Code of Delegated/Subordinate Legislation of Bangladesh
through Identifying and Consolidating All Existing Subordinate Legislations
(Rules, Regulations, By-Laws, Orders, Notifications, etc.) under Package
No. SD-1 (1799-1970) and Package No. SD-5 (2011-till date).

This document outlines our proposed approach, methodology, phased work plan,
Gantt chart, quality assurance framework, risk management strategy, and
deliverables schedule to ensure the successful, timely, and high-quality
completion of this assignment within the project period of
01 June 2026 to 31 May 2027.

The Legal Empire recognizes the critical national importance of this assignment
in consolidating Bangladesh's legislative heritage and making subordinate
legislation accessible, organized, and legally coherent. Our work plan is
designed to address the complexity and scale of both packages simultaneously,
drawing on our team's expertise in historical legal research, legislative
analysis, codification, and digital data management.

2. UNDERSTANDING OF THE ASSIGNMENT
================================================================================

--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
2.1 Background and Context
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------

Bangladesh's legislative landscape encompasses a vast body of delegated and
subordinate legislation — comprising rules, regulations, by-laws, orders,
statutory notifications, and other instruments — enacted under the authority
of parent Acts and Ordinances. These instruments span over two centuries,
from the early colonial period under the East India Company through the
British Crown, the pre-Partition era, the post-Partition period under Pakistan,
and post-Liberation Bangladesh up to the present day.

The Strengthening Legislative Capacity and Legal Awareness Building Project,
under the Legislative and Parliamentary Affairs Division, Ministry of Law,
Justice and Parliamentary Affairs, Government of the People's Republic of
Bangladesh, seeks to systematically identify, compile, consolidate, and codify
this body of subordinate legislation through a structured series of packages.

The Legal Empire has been assigned (subject to selection) to prepare the Code
of Delegated/Subordinate Legislation for the following specific periods:

   PACKAGE SD-1 : Period 1799-1970
   --------------------------------
   This package covers the colonial period, beginning with the early
   regulatory frameworks under the East India Company (1799), through the
   British Crown administration (post-1858), the late colonial period,
   and up to and including 1970 — the year immediately preceding the
   Liberation War of Bangladesh. This period encompasses:
      - Colonial-era regulations and instruments
      - Rules and regulations under pre-Partition British India
      - Post-Partition Pakistan period instruments applicable to the
        territory of present-day Bangladesh
      - Instruments up to December 1970
   Primary sources include the Bengal Gazette, the Gazette of India,
   the Gazette of Pakistan (Eastern Wing), and archival government records.

   PACKAGE SD-5 : Period 2011-till date
   -------------------------------------
   This package covers the contemporary period, from 2011 to the present
   day. This is a high-volume period reflecting Bangladesh's dynamic
   regulatory environment across ministries and departments. Key challenges
   include ensuring comprehensiveness, currency, and the identification of
   amendments, repeals, and overlapping instruments. Primary sources include
   the Bangladesh Gazette (Official and Extraordinary editions), Ministry
   records, departmental registers, and official online repositories.

3. PROPOSED METHODOLOGY
================================================================================

The Legal Empire proposes a structured five-phase methodology aligned with
standard legislative codification best practices and the requirements of the
EOI notice:

--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
PHASE 1: INCEPTION AND PLANNING
Duration: Month 1 (June 2026)
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------

1.1 Inception Meeting with Project Office
    - Formal introduction and briefing session with the Project Director and
      project team
    - Clarification of scope, deliverables, reporting requirements, and
      submission formats
    - Establishment of communication protocols and points of contact

1.2 Detailed Review of Terms of Reference (ToR)
    - Thorough review and internal analysis of the ToR for both packages
    - Identification of specific output requirements, quality standards,
      and submission timelines
    - Alignment of firm's work plan with ToR requirements

1.3 Preparation and Submission of Inception Report
    - Compilation of a comprehensive Inception Report including:
      * Refined work plan and Gantt chart
      * Team mobilization and assignment schedule
      * Data source identification and access strategy
      * Research methodology and tools
      * Quality assurance framework
    - Submission to Project Office for review and approval

1.4 Team Mobilization
    - Full mobilization of all seven (7) key professionals
    - Role and responsibility briefing
    - Assignment of research streams (SD-1 and SD-5) to team members
    - Internal training session on project methodology and standards

1.5 Data Source Mapping
    - Identification and listing of all primary and secondary data sources:
      * Bangladesh National Archives
      * Law Ministry Library and Records Room
      * Bangladesh Gazette archives (print and digital)
      * National Library of Bangladesh
      * Dhaka University Central Library (law section)
      * Relevant Ministries' records rooms
      * Online repositories (for SD-1 colonial instruments)
      * Supreme Court Library
    - Establishment of access protocols and research visit schedules

    KEY DELIVERABLE: Inception Report

--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
PHASE 2: IDENTIFICATION AND COLLECTION
Duration: Months 2-4 (July-September 2026)
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------

2.1 Systematic Identification of Subordinate Legislation

    For Package SD-1 (1799-1970):
    - Systematic review of the Bengal Gazette (historical), Gazette of
      India, Gazette of Pakistan, and archived government records
    - Identification of all rules, regulations, by-laws, orders, and
      notifications enacted during 1799-1970 applicable to present-day
      Bangladesh
    - Review of existing legislative compilations (Acts of Parliament,
      President's Orders, etc.) for enabling provisions that spawned
      subordinate instruments
    - Engagement with the National Archives of Bangladesh for archival
      access to pre-Liberation records

    For Package SD-5 (2011-till date):
    - Systematic review of the Bangladesh Gazette (all editions) from
      2011 to the present
    - Review of official Ministry and Departmental websites and records
    - Identification through the Bangladesh Legal Repository (if available)
      and Ministry of Law's published compilations
    - Consultation with individual Ministries and Departments for
      confirmation and supplementary instruments

2.2 Creation of Master Inventory
    - Development of a structured Master Inventory/Database for each package
    - Each entry to include:
      * Serial Number
      * Title/Name of the Instrument
      * Type (Rules / Regulations / By-Laws / Order / Notification / Other)
      * Parent/Enabling Act/Ordinance
      * Ministry/Department responsible
      * Date of enactment/gazette notification
      * Gazette reference (volume, number, date, page)
      * Current status (Active / Amended / Repealed / Obsolete)
      * Remarks
    - Database to be maintained in structured spreadsheet/database format
      for ongoing update and final submission

2.3 Collection of Authenticated Copies
    - Collection of authenticated gazette copies or certified true copies
      of all identified instruments
    - Scanning and digitization of physical copies where required
    - Organization of collected instruments by package, ministry, and
      chronological order

    KEY DELIVERABLE: Preliminary Inventory Report (Draft) for both packages

--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
PHASE 3: ANALYSIS AND CONSOLIDATION
Duration: Months 4-7 (September-December 2026)
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------

3.1 Legal Analysis of Each Instrument
    - Substantive review of each identified subordinate legislation
    - Analysis to cover:
      * Legal validity and vires (within the scope of enabling legislation)
      * Current operative status (active, partially active, amended, or
        repealed)
      * Date and nature of any amendments
      * Relationship with other instruments (parent Acts, related rules)
    - Annotation of status and legal observations for each instrument

3.2 Classification and Categorization
    - Classification of all instruments by:
      * Ministry / Department / Authority
      * Subject matter / thematic category
        (e.g., Administrative, Civil Service, Commerce, Land Revenue,
         Environment, Finance, Health, etc.)
      * Legal type (Rules, Regulations, By-Laws, Orders, Notifications)
      * Chronological period (within each package)
      * Status (Active / Amended / Repealed / Obsolete)
    - Development of a Classification Schema and Taxonomy for the Code

3.3 Cross-Referencing with Parent Legislation
    - Systematic cross-referencing of each subordinate instrument with
      its parent/enabling Act or Ordinance
    - Preparation of a Parent Legislation Index linking Acts with all
      their associated subordinate instruments

3.4 Gap and Inconsistency Analysis
    - Identification of:
      * Gaps where enabling provisions exist but subordinate instruments
        cannot be located
      * Conflicts or overlaps between instruments
      * Instruments lacking clear legal authority
      * Obsolete instruments that have not been formally repealed
    - Preparation of a Gap and Inconsistency Report for Project Office

3.5 Consolidation of Instruments
    - Consolidation of all verified and classified instruments into a
      structured dataset for codification

    KEY DELIVERABLE: (i) Consolidated Inventory with Legal Analysis,
                     (ii) Gap and Inconsistency Report

--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
PHASE 4: CODIFICATION AND DRAFTING
Duration: Months 7-10 (December 2026 - March 2027)
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------

4.1 Design of the Code Structure
    - Development of the structural framework for the Code of
      Delegated/Subordinate Legislation for each package:
      * Arrangement by Ministry/Department and subject matter
      * Chapter and Part designations
      * Numbering and referencing system
      * Introductory notes, prefaces, and editorial conventions
      * Index and cross-reference tables
    - Submission of structure proposal to Project Office for approval
      before drafting commences

4.2 Preparation of the Code
    - Structured compilation of all verified subordinate legislation
      into the approved Code format
    - Each entry in the Code to include:
      * Full title of the instrument
      * Enabling/parent Act reference
      * Ministry/Department
      * Date of enactment/gazette notification
      * Gazette reference
      * Full text of the instrument (or authenticated excerpt where
        full text is voluminous)
      * Current status annotation
      * Amendment history (if applicable)
    - Separate volumes/sections for SD-1 and SD-5

4.3 Indexing and Referencing
    - Preparation of a comprehensive General Index
    - Ministry-wise Index
    - Subject-matter Index
    - Parent Act / Enabling Legislation Index
    - Chronological Index

4.4 Editorial Review and Internal Quality Check
    - First round of internal quality review by the Quality Assurance
      / Review Specialist
    - Checking for accuracy, completeness, consistent formatting,
      correct citations, and legal precision
    - Correction and revision cycle

    KEY DELIVERABLE: Draft Code of Delegated/Subordinate Legislation
                     (SD-1 and SD-5) -- First Draft

--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
PHASE 5: FINALIZATION AND SUBMISSION
Duration: Months 10-12 (March-May 2027)
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------

5.1 Stakeholder Review and Feedback
    - Submission of First Draft Code to the Project Office for review
    - Presentation of the Draft Code to the Project Director and
      relevant stakeholders
    - Collection and systematic collation of feedback and review comments

5.2 Revision and Finalization
    - Incorporation of all feedback and review comments from the
      Project Office and stakeholders
    - Second round of internal quality assurance review
    - Final editing, formatting, and proofreading of the complete Code

5.3 Preparation of Final Deliverables
    - Preparation of hard copy and digital (soft copy) versions of:
      * Final Code of Delegated/Subordinate Legislation (SD-1 and SD-5)
      * Final Master Inventory (digital database)
      * Gap and Inconsistency Report (final)
      * All supporting indexes and annexures
      * Project Completion Report

5.4 Final Submission and Handover
    - Formal submission of all deliverables to the Project Office
    - Handover of all digital files, databases, and raw research materials
    - Final presentation to the Project Director and Ministry officials
    - Signing of project completion documentation

5.5 Post-Submission Support
    - Availability for clarifications and minor revisions for [X] days
      following final submission
    - Provision of user guidance on the digital database (if required)

    KEY DELIVERABLE: (i) Final Code of Delegated/Subordinate Legislation
                         (SD-1 and SD-5) -- Hard copy and Soft copy
                     (ii) Final Master Inventory Database
                     (iii) All Supporting Indexes and Annexures
                     (iv) Project Completion Report

5. QUALITY ASSURANCE APPROACH
================================================================================

The Legal Empire will apply a rigorous, multi-layered quality assurance (QA)
framework throughout the assignment to ensure accuracy, completeness,
consistency, and legal precision in all outputs.

--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
5.1 QA Framework Structure
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------

   Level 1 - Individual Researcher Review:
   Each researcher is responsible for self-review of their own research
   outputs before submission to the Team Leader. All entries in the Master
   Inventory are to be verified against primary source documents.

   Level 2 - Team Leader Review:
   The Team Leader / Lead Legal Consultant will conduct a structured review
   of all research outputs, legal analyses, and codification drafts before
   compilation. The Team Leader will resolve discrepancies and flag issues
   for escalation.

   Level 3 - Quality Assurance Specialist Review:
   The dedicated Quality Assurance / Review Specialist will conduct an
   independent, comprehensive review of:
      - All entries in the Master Inventory (completeness and accuracy)
      - All legal status determinations (active, amended, repealed)
      - The Draft Code (formatting, citation accuracy, legal precision)
      - Index completeness and consistency
   This review will be conducted prior to submission of each deliverable.

   Level 4 - Peer Review:
   Where feasible and appropriate, selected complex instruments or legal
   analyses will be subject to peer review by a second legal professional
   within the team.

   Level 5 - Project Office Review:
   Submission of draft deliverables to the Project Office for their review
   and feedback, with structured revision and response cycle.

--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
5.2 QA Standards
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
   - All citations to cite full gazette reference (volume, number, date, page)
   - All status determinations to be supported by documentary evidence
   - Consistent formatting throughout (as per approved template)
   - Zero tolerance for factual inaccuracies in legal citations
   - All amendments and repeal notes to cross-reference the amending/
     repealing instrument
