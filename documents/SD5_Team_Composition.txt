1. OVERVIEW
================================================================================

The Legal Empire proposes a dedicated team of seven (7) qualified key
professionals for Package SD-5, supported by administrative and IT support
staff. The team has been specifically composed to address the scope and
complexity of this assignment covering contemporary subordinate legislation
from 2011 to the present day.

The team structure ensures:
   - Strong leadership through a retired Additional Secretary (Drafting)
     of LPAD with 40+ years of legislative drafting experience
   - Deep judicial and legal drafting expertise from retired Senior District
     Judges and Additional Secretaries
   - International legal scholarship from a Lincoln's Inn Barrister and
     Associate Professor with extensive research credentials
   - Dedicated data management and IT support capability
   - Independent quality assurance from an academic with post-doctoral
     research experience
