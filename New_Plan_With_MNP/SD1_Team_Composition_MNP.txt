1. OVERVIEW
================================================================================

MNP Legal proposes a dedicated team of seven (7) qualified key professionals
for Package SD-1 (1799-1970), supported by the firm's administrative and IT
infrastructure. The team has been specifically composed to address the scope,
complexity, and historical nature of this assignment covering the colonial
period through to 1970.

--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
  Name                 :  Mst. Lutfa Begum

  - Prepare preliminary inventory entries for all identified instruments
  - Conduct substantive legal analysis of identified legislation
  - Determine and document legal status of each instrument
  - Cross-reference subordinate instruments with parent/enabling Acts

--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
  Name                 :  Hosne Ara Begum

  Responsibilities     :

  - Design and finalize structural framework and format of the Code
  - Draft, compile, and edit all Code entries with proper legal language
  - Ensure legal precision and drafting consistency
  - Prepare introductory sections, editorial notes, and annotations
  - Develop and apply consistent editorial conventions and style guide

--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
PROFESSIONAL 4: LEGAL ANALYST / CODIFICATION EXPERT
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
  Name                 :  Ayesha Saleh

  - Maintain and update Master Inventory Database
  - Identify overlaps, conflicts, and gaps
  - Prepare Gap and Inconsistency Report
  - Prepare all indexes (General, Ministry-wise, Subject-matter, etc.)

  - Prepare preliminary draft entries for the Master Inventory
  - Assist in data verification and cross-checking tasks
