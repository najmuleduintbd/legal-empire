--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
SABEL NAWAZ
Barrister-at-Law, Advocate, Supreme Court of Bangladesh
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------

Sabel Nawaz is a Barrister-at-Law from The Honourable Society of Lincoln's
Inn and an Advocate of the Supreme Court of Bangladesh. He completed his LLB
degree from the University of Reading and finished his Bar Professional
Training Course from Bristol Institute of Legal Practice, University of the
West of England. Mr. Nawaz regularly appears in all jurisdictions of the
Supreme Court of Bangladesh, conducts hearings in Writs, Company matters,
Civil, Criminal and Admiralty Cases. He also appears before the Appellate
Division of the Supreme Court, Sub-ordinate Courts, Administrative Tribunals,
Appeal Tribunal and Bankruptcy Court. He possesses skills of taking part in
international commercial arbitrations both at home and abroad.

--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
EHSAN E MOAZZAM
Barrister-at-Law, Advocate, District & Session Judge Court, Dhaka
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------

Ehsan E Moazzam is a Barrister-at-Law from The Honourable Society of Lincoln's
Inn. He gained his Bachelor of Laws (LL.B) from the London Metropolitan
University and his Masters of Laws (LL.M) specializing in International
Business Law from BPP Law School in London. He completed his Bar Professional
Training Course from BPP Law School, London and was called to the Bar in
November 2015. Currently, besides practicing law profusely he is also serving
as the Chief Legal Advisor in South Bangla Agriculture and Commerce Bank
Limited (SBAC) and has gained considerable experience in Banking, Financial
and Land laws of Bangladesh.
