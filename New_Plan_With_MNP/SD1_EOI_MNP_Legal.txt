7. APPROACH AND METHODOLOGY (BRIEF)
================================================================================

To fulfill the objectives of this assignment under Package No. SD-1
(1799-1970), MNP Legal proposes the following five-phase approach:

Phase 1: Inception and Planning (Month 1)
   - Detailed review of Terms of Reference (ToR)
   - Development of comprehensive work plan and methodology
   - Identification of data sources: Bengal Gazette, Gazette of India,
     Gazette of Pakistan, National Archives, colonial-era repositories
   - Stakeholder consultation and coordination with the Project Office

Phase 2: Identification and Collection (Months 2-4)
   - Systematic identification of all delegated/subordinate legislations
     enacted during the period 1799-1970 applicable to present-day
     Bangladesh
   - Collection from official gazettes, government archives, ministry
     records, and other authoritative sources
   - Creation of a master inventory/database of identified legislations
   - Research streams covering: East India Company period (1799-1858),
     British Crown Rule (1858-1947), Pakistan Period (1947-1970)

Phase 3: Analysis and Consolidation (Months 4-7)
   - Legal analysis of each identified subordinate legislation
   - Classification by ministry, subject matter, legal authority, and status
   - Cross-referencing with parent/enabling Acts
   - Identification of inconsistencies, overlaps, and gaps
   - Gap and Inconsistency Report

Phase 4: Codification and Drafting (Months 7-10)
   - Preparation of the Code of Delegated/Subordinate Legislation
   - Structured compilation organized by ministry and subject matter
   - Comprehensive indexing (General, Ministry-wise, Subject-matter,
     Parent Act, and Chronological indexes)
   - Quality review and peer verification

Phase 5: Finalization and Submission (Months 10-12)
   - Stakeholder review and feedback incorporation
   - Final submission of the Code (hard and soft copy)
   - All supporting documentation and databases
   - Final presentation to the Project Office
