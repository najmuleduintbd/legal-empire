NOTES
================================================================================

1. Documents are organized by the year of their enactment/promulgation.
2. Where the exact year is ambiguous (e.g., compilations spanning multiple
   years), documents are placed in the earliest applicable year folder.
3. Large compilation volumes (Bengal Code, Calcutta Gazette) contain
   legislation spanning multiple years within a single PDF.
4. The Bengal Code Volumes (I, II, and Supplement) together contain the
   complete text of Bengal Regulations from 1793 to 1923.
5. Pakistan Central Acts collections from molaw.gov.pk cover federal
   legislation applicable to East Pakistan (1947-1970).
