the President (1956-1958, 1962-1969), or under Martial Law authority.

than Acts.

NOTES ON COMPLETENESS
================================================================================

1. BDLAWS DATABASE: The bdlaws.minlaw.gov.bd database lists approximately
   1,300+ laws currently in its database, of which a significant portion
   (estimated 300-400) originate from the 1947-1970 period. This compilation
   captures the most significant legislation but the full list requires
   systematic verification against the bdlaws database.

2. GAZETTE REVIEW: A comprehensive identification requires systematic
   review of:
   - The Gazette of Pakistan (1947-1971) - all extraordinary issues
   - The East Bengal/East Pakistan Gazette (1947-1971) - all extraordinary
     issues
   - Both ordinary and extraordinary issues contain notifications of
     new legislation, amendments, and orders

3. ESTIMATED TOTAL UNIVERSE: Legal scholars estimate that between 2,000
   and 5,000 distinct legislative instruments (including all Acts,
   Ordinances, Orders, MLOs, MLRs, SROs, notifications, and rules)
   were applicable to East Pakistan during 1947-1970. This compilation
   identifies approximately 450 of the most significant.

4. ANNUAL FINANCE ACTS: Pakistan enacted annual Finance Acts from 1947
   onwards, each containing tax amendments. These are not individually
   listed but are significant legislative instruments.

5. APPROPRIATION ACTS: Annual Appropriation Acts authorizing government
   expenditure are not individually listed.

6. AMENDMENT ACTS: Many Acts had multiple amendment Acts during this
   period. Only the principal Acts are listed; individual amendment
   Acts are not separately enumerated.

7. WEST PAKISTAN ACTS: Some West Pakistan Acts were later extended to
   East Pakistan by presidential/governor's orders. These are not
   exhaustively captured here.

KEY LEGISLATION STILL ACTIVELY APPLIED IN BANGLADESH (2026)
================================================================================

The following legislation from the 1947-1970 period remains critically
important in Bangladesh's daily legal and administrative practice:

TIER 1 - FOUNDATIONAL (applied daily across Bangladesh):

1.  East Bengal State Acquisition and Tenancy Act, 1950 (EA-04)
    - Governs ALL land administration in Bangladesh
    - Applied in: Land offices, courts, AC Land offices nationwide

2.  Muslim Family Laws Ordinance, 1961 (CO-05)
    - Governs ALL Muslim marriages, divorces, maintenance
    - Applied in: Union Councils, Family Courts, Kazi offices

3.  Government Servants (Conduct) Rules, 1964 (under various Acts)
    - Governs conduct of all government servants
    - Applied in: All government offices

4.  Code of Criminal Procedure, 1898 (PR-02)
    - Primary criminal procedural law
    - Applied in: All criminal courts

5.  Code of Civil Procedure, 1908 (PR-03)
    - Primary civil procedural law
    - Applied in: All civil courts

6.  Penal Code, 1860 (PR-01)
    - Primary criminal law
    - Applied in: All criminal courts
