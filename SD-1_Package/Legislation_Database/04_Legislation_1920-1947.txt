NOTES
================================================================================

1. STATUS DEFINITIONS:
   - "In force (with amendments)": The Act continues to apply in Bangladesh,
     typically adapted through the Bangladesh Laws Revision and Declaration
     Order, 1973 (President's Order No. 48 of 1973) and the Bangladesh
     (Adaptation of Existing Laws) Order, 1972 (President's Order No. 48 of 1972).
   - "Repealed": Explicitly repealed by subsequent legislation.
   - "Superseded": Replaced by newer legislation covering the same subject.
   - "Spent": Temporary legislation whose purpose has been fulfilled.
   - "Merged": Amendments that were consolidated into the parent Act.
   - "Not applicable": Legislation that never applied to Bengal/East Pakistan/Bangladesh.

2. LEGAL CONTINUITY:
   Under Article 149 of the Bangladesh Constitution and the Laws Continuance
   Enforcement Order, 1971, all laws in force in East Pakistan immediately
   before independence on 26 March 1971 continue to be in force in Bangladesh
   until amended or repealed by competent authority.

3. ADAPTATION:
   Many pre-independence laws were adapted through:
   - Adaptation of Central Acts and Ordinances Order, 1949
   - Bangladesh (Adaptation of Existing Laws) Order, 1972
   - Bangladesh Laws (Revision and Declaration) Order, 1973

4. BENGAL PARTITION (1947):
   After the partition of Bengal in 1947, the laws of undivided Bengal
   continued to apply to East Bengal (later East Pakistan, then Bangladesh)
   and were adapted as necessary.

5. This list attempts to be comprehensive but cannot claim absolute
   completeness. Colonial-era legislative records are extensive, and some
   minor amending Acts, temporary ordinances, and wartime emergency
   measures may not be individually listed.
