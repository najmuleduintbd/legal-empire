================================================================================
COMPREHENSIVE LIST OF LEGISLATION (1947-1970) APPLICABLE TO EAST PAKISTAN /
PRESENT-DAY BANGLADESH
================================================================================
Compiled for Legal Empire - SD-1 Package
Coverage: Pakistan Central Acts, East Pakistan/East Bengal Provincial Acts,
Ordinances, Martial Law Orders/Regulations, Presidential Orders (1947-1970)
================================================================================

ABBREVIATIONS:
  C   = Central (Pakistan)
  P   = Provincial (East Pakistan / East Bengal)
  MLO = Martial Law Order
  MLR = Martial Law Regulation
  PO  = Presidential Order
  GO  = Governor's Order
  Ord = Ordinance
  SA  = Still Applicable (with amendments) in Bangladesh
  R   = Repealed
  S   = Superseded
  PA  = Partially Applicable
  AM  = Amended and Applicable

NOTE ON STATUS: Many of these laws were continued in force in Bangladesh by
virtue of the Laws Continuance Enforcement Order 1971 (President's Order No. 48
of 1972), the Bangladesh (Adaptation of Existing Laws) Order 1972 (President's
Order No. 48), and Article 149 of the Constitution of Bangladesh 1972.

NOTES AND CAVEATS
================================================================================

1. COVERAGE: This list attempts to be as comprehensive as possible based on
   available knowledge. The actual number of laws, ordinances, martial law
   orders and regulations from this period exceeds what can be fully catalogued
   here. Provincial gazette records would contain additional minor amendments,
   notifications, and rules.

2. ADAPTATION: Many pre-1947 (British India) laws were adapted through various
   Adaptation Orders (1947, 1948, 1950, 1956, 1962). The adapted versions
   became the effective law in Pakistan and later Bangladesh.

3. CONTINUANCE IN BANGLADESH: After independence in 1971, laws in force were
   continued by:
   - Laws Continuance Enforcement Order, 1971
   - Bangladesh (Adaptation of Existing Laws) Order, 1972 (P.O. No. 48)
   - Article 149 of the Constitution of Bangladesh, 1972
   Many were subsequently amended, replaced, or repealed by Bangladesh
   legislation.

4. STATUS INDICATORS:
   - SA = Still Applicable (the law or its substantially amended version
     remains in force in Bangladesh)
   - PA = Partially Applicable (some provisions remain, others repealed/amended)
   - R  = Repealed (no longer in force)
   - S  = Superseded (replaced by later legislation)
   - Historical = Of historical/constitutional significance only

5. ACT/ORDINANCE NUMBERS: Where specific Act or Ordinance numbers could not be
   confirmed from available records, the field has been marked "--". Researchers
   should consult the Bangladesh Code, the Pakistan Code, and the East Pakistan
   Gazette for exact numbering.

6. MARTIAL LAW PERIODS:
   - First Martial Law: 7 October 1958 - 8 June 1962
   - Second Martial Law: 25 March 1969 - 20 December 1971 (in East Pakistan,
     effectively ended with Bangladesh independence)

7. EAST BENGAL vs EAST PAKISTAN: The province was known as "East Bengal" from
   1947 to 1955, and was renamed "East Pakistan" in 1955. Laws from both
   periods are included under the unified heading of East Pakistan/East Bengal.
