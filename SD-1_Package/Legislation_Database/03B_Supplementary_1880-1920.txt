PRESENT-DAY BANGLADESH

Compiled from: bdlaws.minlaw.gov.bd, India Code (indiacode.nic.in),
Indian Kanoon, Wikipedia, and legal databases.

NOTE: "In force in BD" status is based on the Bangladesh Code as maintained
by the Legislative and Parliamentary Affairs Division (bdlaws.minlaw.gov.bd).
Acts listed on bdlaws are indicated as "Yes" (still in force, possibly with
amendments). Acts repealed or not adopted are marked accordingly.

13. Title: The Bengal Tenancy Act, 1885
    Act No: Bengal Act VIII of 1885
    Year: 1885
    Category: Land Tenancy / Agrarian Law
    In force in BD: Yes (as amended extensively; bdlaws)
    Notes: Landmark legislation governing landlord-tenant relations in
    Bengal. Defined rights of ryots (cultivators), occupancy rights,
    rent fixation, and transfer of holdings. Later amended by numerous
    Bengal and Pakistan/Bangladesh amendments. Still partially in force
    in Bangladesh (significant provisions remain operative for tenancy
    disputes predating the State Acquisition and Tenancy Act, 1950).

60. Title: The Code of Civil Procedure, 1908
    Act No: Act V of 1908
    Year: 1908
    Category: Civil Procedure
    In force in BD: Yes (BD Act V of 1908, bdlaws)
    Notes: THE principal civil procedural statute of Bangladesh. Governs
    all civil court proceedings including suits, appeals, execution of
    decrees, and all ancillary matters. Contains both the body of the
    Code and extensive Orders and Rules in the First Schedule.

61. Title: The Indian Registration Act, 1908
    Act No: Act XVI of 1908
    Year: 1908

    In force in BD: Yes (BD Act XVI of 1908, bdlaws)
    Notes: Governs registration of documents, particularly those
    relating to immovable property. Mandatory registration of sale
    deeds, leases over a year, etc.

62. Title: The Indian Limitation Act, 1908
    Act No: Act IX of 1908
    Year: 1908
    Category: Limitation of Suits

NOTES AND CAVEATS
================================================================================

1. EXHAUSTIVENESS: This list is compiled from my training data which includes
   the bdlaws.minlaw.gov.bd database, India Code, Indian Kanoon, and
   Wikipedia. However, I was unable to perform LIVE web searches (both
   WebSearch and WebFetch tools were denied permission). For a truly
   exhaustive list, I strongly recommend:

   a) Browsing bdlaws.minlaw.gov.bd/laws-of-bangladesh/volume-1/ directly
      — Volume I of Bangladesh Laws contains all pre-1947 legislation
      still in force, organized chronologically.

   b) Searching indiacode.nic.in with year filters for 1880-1920 to
      cross-reference Central Acts.

   c) Consulting the "Chronological List of Central Acts" published by
      the Legislative Department, Government of India.

2. BENGAL PROVINCIAL ACTS: Many Bengal local Acts were not codified in
   central databases. The Bengal Legislative Council's proceedings
   (1862-1920) contain additional minor legislation on topics like:
   - Local rates and cesses
   - Market regulation
   - Specific district administration
   - Ferry and road tolls
   - Local sanitation and drainage

3. "IN FORCE IN BANGLADESH" STATUS: Bangladesh inherited all pre-1947
   British Indian legislation through:
   - Indian Independence Act, 1947
   - Pakistan (Adaptation of Laws) Order, 1947
   - Laws Continuance Enforcement Order, 1971
   - Bangladesh Laws (Revision and Declaration) Act, 1973

   Acts remain in force unless expressly repealed or replaced.

4. IMPORTANT OMISSIONS TO VERIFY: The following may have additional
   entries discoverable only through live database searches:
   - Minor Bengal Regulations (pre-1834 style) still technically in force
   - Revenue Regulations specific to Bengal divisions
   - Municipal and local board regulations for specific districts
