================================================================================
COMPREHENSIVE LEGISLATION DATABASE: 1880-1920
Applicable to Bengal / Present-Day Bangladesh
================================================================================

Compiled from training knowledge. Covers Acts of the Indian Legislature
(Governor-General in Council), Bengal Provincial Acts, and relevant UK
Parliament Acts. Period: 1880-1920.

Format:
S/N | Year | Title | Type | Act No. | Subject Category | Status in Bangladesh

NOTE ON STATUS:
  "In Force" = Still operative in Bangladesh (with or without amendments)
  "In Force (Amended)" = Operative with significant amendments
  "Repealed" = Expressly repealed in Bangladesh / East Pakistan
  "Obsolete" = Not formally repealed but effectively dead letter
  "Superseded" = Replaced by later legislation
  "Partially In Force" = Some provisions still operative
  "Uncertain" = Status not definitively confirmed

NOTES

1. This list is compiled from training knowledge and is intended to be as
   comprehensive as possible. However, minor Acts, temporary Acts, and some
   amending Acts may not be individually listed.

2. The "Status in Bangladesh" column reflects the position as understood
   up to early 2025. Some Acts may have been amended or repealed by
   subsequent Bangladesh legislation not captured here.

3. Many pre-1880 Acts (Indian Penal Code 1860, Evidence Act 1872, Contract
   Act 1872, etc.) continued to be extensively amended during 1880-1920 and
   remain in force in Bangladesh. They are listed in Section B for
   completeness.

4. The Bengal Tenancy Act, 1885 — while repealed by the State Acquisition
   and Tenancy Act, 1950 — shaped the entire land tenure system of
   Bangladesh and its provisions continue to influence judicial
   interpretation.

5. Bangladesh inherited all pre-1947 legislation through the Indian
   Independence Act 1947, and all pre-1971 Pakistan legislation through
   the Bangladesh Laws (Revision and Declaration) Act, 1973 and
   Article 149 of the Bangladesh Constitution.
