================================================================================
COMPREHENSIVE LEGISLATION DATABASE: 1834-1880
APPLICABLE TO BENGAL / PRESENT-DAY BANGLADESH
================================================================================

Period: From the Charter Act of 1833 (effective 1834) through 1880
Scope: Acts of the Governor-General in Council, Acts of the Indian Legislature,
       Bengal-specific Acts, Presidency Acts, and relevant UK Parliament Acts
       applicable to Bengal and the territories of present-day Bangladesh.

Note on Legislative Authority:
- 1834-1853: Governor-General in Council legislated for all of British India
  (Charter Act 1833 centralized legislative power)
- 1854-1861: Legislative Council of India (expanded under Charter Act 1853)
- 1861 onwards: Indian Councils Act 1861 created provincial legislative councils;
  Bengal Legislative Council established; Acts of the Governor-General in Council
  continued alongside Bengal Acts.

Format: S/N | Year | Title | Type | Act No. | Subject Category | Status in Bangladesh

Legend for Type:
  GG   = Act of Governor-General in Council
  IND  = Act of the Indian Legislature / Legislative Council of India
  BENG = Bengal Act (Provincial Legislature)
  UK   = Act of the UK Parliament

Legend for Status in Bangladesh:
  In Force        = Still in force (with or without amendments)
  Repealed        = Repealed by subsequent legislation
  Replaced        = Replaced by later enactment
  Partially       = Partially in force / partially repealed
  Adapted         = Adapted post-1947/1971 with modifications
  Obsolete        = No longer operative though not formally repealed
  Uncertain       = Status uncertain or not clearly determined

NOTES ON SOURCES AND METHODOLOGY
================================================================================

1. This list is compiled from knowledge of the published Acts of the Indian
   Legislature, Bengal Provincial Legislature, and UK Parliament Acts relating
   to India during 1834-1880.

2. The numbering of early Acts (pre-1860) is sometimes inconsistent in
   historical sources. Act numbers are given where confidently known; otherwise
   the year and descriptive title are provided.

3. "Precursor" entries are included where significant legislative processes
   (Law Commission drafts, committee reports) were underway that led to
   enacted legislation in later years. These are included for completeness
   of the legislative history but are not Acts in themselves.

4. Status in Bangladesh reflects the position as generally understood. Many
   colonial-era Acts were retained through the Laws Continuance Enforcement
   Order 1971 (Bangladesh) and subsequent adaptation orders.

5. The Government of Bangladesh has periodically consolidated, amended, and
   repealed colonial legislation. The status given here reflects general
   understanding and may not capture every minor amendment.

6. Bengal-specific Acts are particularly important for present-day Bangladesh
   as East Bengal (later East Pakistan) inherited the Bengal provincial
   legislative corpus.
