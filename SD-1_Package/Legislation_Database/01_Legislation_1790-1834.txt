================================================================================
PART B: BENGAL REGULATIONS — YEAR BY YEAR (1793-1834)
================================================================================

Note on Bengal Regulations:
- The systematic codification began under Governor-General Lord Cornwallis in 1793.
- Regulations were numbered sequentially within each year (e.g., Regulation I of 1793).
- The total corpus runs to approximately 700+ regulations through 1834.
- After 1834, the legislative power shifted to the Governor-General in Council
  for all of British India under the Charter Act of 1833.

NOTES AND CAVEATS
================================================================================

1. COMPLETENESS: This database represents the most comprehensive listing
   possible from available historical knowledge. The exact total count of
   Bengal Regulations varies between sources (Colebrooke's Digest, Harington's
   Analysis, the official Bengal Code). Some regulations were purely
   administrative circulars elevated to regulation status.

2. NUMBERING: The Bengal Regulations used Roman numeral numbering within each
   year (e.g., Regulation VII of 1822). Some years had supplementary
   regulations that were numbered non-sequentially or added mid-year.

3. APPLICABILITY TO BANGLADESH: After the Partition of Bengal (1947) and the
   independence of Bangladesh (1971), the legal position is:
   - The East Bengal State Acquisition and Tenancy Act 1950 effectively
     replaced the entire Permanent Settlement framework.
   - Criminal law was replaced by the Penal Code 1860 and CrPC 1898.
   - Civil procedure was replaced by the Code of Civil Procedure 1908.
   - Police law was replaced by the Police Act 1861.
   - Most Bengal Regulations were formally or effectively repealed through
     the Repealing and Amending Acts of the 19th and 20th centuries.

4. KEY REPEALING LEGISLATION:
   - Bengal Regulations Repeal Act (various years in the 1870s-1880s)
   - Repealing and Amending Act, 1891
   - Repealing and Amending Act, 1914
   - Repealing and Amending Act, 1927
   - Laws Continuance Enforcement Order, 1971 (Bangladesh)
   - Bangladesh Laws Revision and Declaration Act, 1973

5. SOURCES: This compilation draws on:
   - Colebrooke's Supplement to the Digest of Bengal Regulations
   - Harington's Analysis of the Bengal Regulations
   - The official Bengal Code (published by the Government of Bengal)
   - Morley's Digest of Indian Cases
   - Ilbert's The Government of India (historical analysis)
   - Various historical and legal scholarship on colonial Bengal law
