PREFACE
================================================================================

Volume II of the Code covers the most prolific period for subordinate
legislation in Bangladesh's legal history: 1858-1947, spanning nearly ninety
years of British Crown rule. Following the transfer of power from the East
India Company to the British Crown in 1858, Bengal saw the establishment of
a modern legislative framework, with extensive rule-making powers delegated
to both central and provincial governments.

This period witnessed the enactment of foundational statutes — the Indian
Penal Code (1860), Code of Criminal Procedure (1898), Code of Civil Procedure
(1908), and numerous Bengal-specific Acts — each generating substantial bodies
of subordinate legislation. The Government of India Acts of 1919 and 1935
further expanded provincial rule-making authority, leading to a significant
increase in Bengal-specific subordinate legislation.

Many subordinate legislations from this era remain partially in force in
Bangladesh today, making this volume directly relevant to contemporary legal
practice and administration.

CHAPTER 1: CRIMINAL LAW, JUSTICE, AND POLICE
================================================================================

OVERVIEW:
The criminal justice system in Bengal during 1858-1947 was governed primarily
by three foundational statutes: the Indian Penal Code, 1860; the Code of
Criminal Procedure, 1898; and the Indian Evidence Act, 1872. Each generated
extensive subordinate legislation in the form of rules, circulars, and orders.
The police administration was governed by the Police Act, 1861, and the
comprehensive Police Regulations of Bengal.

--------------------------------------------------------------------------------

1.1   BENGAL CRIMINAL RULES AND ORDERS
      (Practice and Procedure of Criminal Courts)

      Parent Act           : Code of Criminal Procedure, 1898 (Act V of 1898)
      Date                 : 1898 (comprehensively revised periodically)
      Authority            : High Court of Calcutta / Government of Bengal
      Type                 : Rules
      Gazette Reference    : Bengal Gazette (various issues)

      PROVISIONS:
      Part I   — General Rules
        - Organization and jurisdiction of criminal courts
        - Assignment of cases and distribution of work
        - Court hours, sittings, and calendar
        - Dress code for courts

      Part II  — Investigation and Inquiry
        - Police investigation procedures
        - Magisterial inquiry procedure
        - Recording of statements and confessions
        - Search and seizure procedures
        - First Information Report (FIR) requirements

      Part III — Trial Procedure
        - Charge framing
        - Examination of witnesses
        - Documentary evidence
        - Arguments and judgments
        - Sentencing guidelines

      Part IV  — Appeals, Revisions, and References
        - Appeal filing procedure
        - Revision jurisdiction
        - Reference to High Court
        - Bail procedure (original and appellate)

      Part V   — Execution of Sentences
        - Imprisonment execution
        - Fine recovery
        - Death sentence execution
        - Probation and release

      Part VI  — Miscellaneous
        - Record maintenance and destruction
        - Court statistics and returns
        - Transfer of cases
        - Contempt of court

      STATUS: PARTIALLY IN FORCE IN BANGLADESH
      - Adapted for East Pakistan and subsequently Bangladesh
      - Forms the basis of criminal court practice in Bangladesh
      - Known as "Criminal Rules and Orders (Practice and Procedure)"

      CROSS-REFERENCES:
      - Police Regulations of Bengal (see 1.3)
      - Bengal Jail Code (see Chapter 13)
      - Evidence Act rules

      SIGNIFICANCE FOR BANGLADESH:
      These rules, with adaptations, continue to govern the day-to-day
      functioning of criminal courts in Bangladesh. They are essential
      reference for all criminal practitioners and judicial officers.

--------------------------------------------------------------------------------

1.2   HIGH COURT CRIMINAL CIRCULARS AND ORDERS (BENGAL)

      Parent Act           : Code of Criminal Procedure, 1898
      Date                 : Various (1862-1947)
      Authority            : High Court of Calcutta
      Type                 : Circulars and Orders

      DESCRIPTION:
      A comprehensive body of practice directions issued by the High Court
      of Calcutta to subordinate criminal courts in Bengal. These covered:
      - Interpretation of statutory provisions
      - Standard procedures for specific types of cases
      - Sentencing guidelines
      - Administrative directions for court management
      - Directions on handling of specific offences

      STATUS: Historical / Partially superseded
      - Many principles incorporated into later practice manuals

--------------------------------------------------------------------------------

1.3   POLICE REGULATIONS OF BENGAL (PRB)

      Parent Act           : Police Act, 1861 (Act V of 1861)
      Date                 : 1861 (comprehensively revised 1910, 1943)
      Authority            : Government of Bengal
      Type                 : Regulations
      Gazette Reference    : Bengal Gazette (published as multi-volume manual)

      PROVISIONS:
      Volume I — Organization and Administration
        Chapter 1  : General principles of police organization
        Chapter 2  : Recruitment and appointment
        Chapter 3  : Training
        Chapter 4  : Ranks, grades, and pay
        Chapter 5  : Discipline and punishment
        Chapter 6  : Leave, transfer, and retirement
        Chapter 7  : Police stations and jurisdiction
        Chapter 8  : Reporting hierarchy

      Volume II — Duties and Procedure
        Chapter 9  : Prevention of crime
        Chapter 10 : Investigation of offences
        Chapter 11 : Arrest, search, and seizure
        Chapter 12 : Handling of property
        Chapter 13 : Diary and record keeping
        Chapter 14 : Relations with magistracy
        Chapter 15 : Public order and crowd control
        Chapter 16 : Traffic and transport regulation

      Volume III — Special Subjects
        Chapter 17 : Arms and explosives
        Chapter 18 : Jail escorts and prisoners
        Chapter 19 : Intelligence and political matters
        Chapter 20 : Finger printing and identification
        Chapter 21 : Prosecution management
        Chapter 22 : Police welfare and morale

      STATUS: PARTIALLY IN FORCE IN BANGLADESH
      - Adapted as East Pakistan Police Rules and subsequently Bangladesh
        Police Rules
      - Remains the foundational reference for police administration

      HISTORICAL NOTES:
      The PRB is one of the most extensive and detailed bodies of
      subordinate legislation in Bengal. In its various editions, it ran
      to several hundred pages across multiple volumes. Its influence on
      policing practice continues to this day.

--------------------------------------------------------------------------------

1.4   RULES UNDER THE IDENTIFICATION OF PRISONERS ACT, 1920

      Parent Act           : Identification of Prisoners Act, 1920
      Date                 : 1920
      Authority            : Government of Bengal
      Type                 : Rules

      PROVISIONS:
      - Fingerprinting procedures for arrested persons
      - Photography of prisoners
      - Measurement and identification records
      - Maintenance of identification records
      - Inter-district sharing of identification data

      STATUS: Partially in force

--------------------------------------------------------------------------------

1.5   ARMS RULES (BENGAL)

      Parent Act           : Indian Arms Act, 1878 / Arms Act, 1878
      Date                 : 1878 (revised 1924, 1937)
      Authority            : Government of Bengal
      Type                 : Rules

      PROVISIONS:
      - Licensing of firearms and ammunition
      - Conditions for arms possession
      - Exemptions (government servants, military)
      - Arms dealers licensing
      - Import and export of arms
      - Penalties for violation

      STATUS: Superseded by later Arms Rules

2.1   BENGAL CIVIL RULES AND ORDERS
      (Practice and Procedure of Civil Courts)

      Parent Act           : Code of Civil Procedure, 1908 (Act V of 1908)
      Date                 : 1908 (amended periodically)
      Authority            : High Court of Calcutta / Government of Bengal
      Type                 : Rules

      PROVISIONS:
      Part I   — Institution of Suits
        - Plaint presentation and verification
        - Court fee requirements
        - Jurisdiction and forum determination
        - Cause title and parties

      Part II  — Interlocutory Proceedings
        - Temporary injunctions
        - Appointment of receivers
        - Attachment before judgment
        - Discovery and inspection
        - Commission and interrogatories

      Part III — Trial and Hearing
        - Framing of issues
        - Examination of witnesses
        - Documentary evidence admission
        - Arguments and judgment writing

      Part IV  — Decree Execution
        - Execution application procedure
        - Attachment and sale of property
        - Arrest and detention of judgment debtor
        - Distribution of assets

      Part V   — Appeals and Revision
        - Appeal filing and procedure
        - Second appeal to High Court
        - Revision jurisdiction

      Part VI  — Special Proceedings
        - Probate and administration
        - Guardianship proceedings
        - Insolvency proceedings
        - Partition suits

      STATUS: PARTIALLY IN FORCE IN BANGLADESH
      - Adapted for East Pakistan and Bangladesh
      - Continues to govern civil court procedure
