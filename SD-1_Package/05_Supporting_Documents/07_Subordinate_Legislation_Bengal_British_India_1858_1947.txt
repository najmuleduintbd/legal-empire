================================================================================
        SUBORDINATE/DELEGATED LEGISLATION OF BENGAL/BRITISH INDIA (1858-1947)
        RELEVANT TO PRESENT-DAY BANGLADESH

        Comprehensive Research Compilation for Package SD-1
        Prepared for: Code of Delegated/Subordinate Legislation of Bangladesh
        Project: Strengthening Legislative Capacity and Legal Awareness Building
================================================================================

NOTE ON METHODOLOGY:
This document compiles subordinate/delegated legislation (rules, regulations,
by-laws, orders, notifications) enacted during British India (1858-1947) under
parent/enabling Acts, with focus on Bengal Presidency / Bengal Province as the
principal jurisdiction relevant to present-day Bangladesh. Entries are organized
by subject-matter category. Status indications are based on known legislative
history; final verification against the Bangladesh Gazette and bdlaws.minlaw.gov.bd
is required for each instrument.

ABBREVIATIONS:
  BG   = Bengal Gazette / Calcutta Gazette
  GI   = Gazette of India
  GoB  = Government of Bengal
  GoI  = Government of India
  SRO  = Statutory Rules and Orders
  SOR  = Statutory Order
  BTA  = Bengal Tenancy Act
  IPC  = Indian Penal Code
  CrPC = Code of Criminal Procedure
  CPC  = Code of Civil Procedure

CATEGORY 1: CRIMINAL LAW AND PROCEDURE -- RULES AND REGULATIONS
================================================================================

1.1  Title: Rules under the Code of Criminal Procedure, 1898 (Act V of 1898)
     Year: 1898 onwards (various notifications)
     Type: Rules
     Parent Act: Code of Criminal Procedure, 1898
     Subject: Criminal procedure -- court processes, bail, summons, warrants,
              trial procedures, execution of sentences
     Sub-instruments:
       (a) Criminal Rules and Orders (Bengal) -- also known as
           "Criminal Rules and Orders (Practice and Procedure of the
           Criminal Courts in Bengal)"
           - Originally framed by the High Court of Calcutta
           - Multiple editions and amendments through 1947
           - Status: Substantially active in Bangladesh (as adapted);
             forms the basis of the Criminal Rules and Orders still
             used by Bangladesh courts
       (b) Rules for Identification of Prisoners
           - Year: 1920
           - Parent Act: Identification of Prisoners Act, 1920 (Act XXXIII of 1920)
           - Type: Rules
           - Status: Active (as adapted) until replaced by modern legislation
       (c) Rules regarding Execution of Death Sentences
           - Framed under CrPC sections
           - Type: Rules / Standing Orders
           - Status: Active (as adapted)

1.2  Title: Indian Penal Code -- Related Executive Rules and Notifications
     Year: 1860 onwards
     Type: Notifications / Orders
     Parent Act: Indian Penal Code, 1860 (Act XLV of 1860)
     Subject: Declarations under various IPC sections (e.g., Section 21
              definitions of "public servant," Section 99 notifications)
     Note: The IPC itself is a primary Act; subordinate instruments include
           GoI and GoB notifications specifying categories, declaring
           offences under specific provisions, etc.
     Status: Many notifications subsumed into subsequent amendments;
             verification required

1.3  Title: Bengal Police Regulations
     Year: 1905 (original), revised through 1943
     Type: Regulations
     Parent Act: Police Act, 1861 (Act V of 1861); Indian Police Act, 1861
     Subject: Organization, discipline, duties, and powers of police in Bengal
     Key Contents:
       - Regulation I to XXXVI covering all aspects of police administration
       - Recruitment and service conditions
       - Investigation procedures
       - Maintenance of records
       - Use of force protocols
     Status: Substantially active in Bangladesh as "Police Regulations of
             Bengal" (PRB); still the primary regulatory framework for
             Bangladesh Police alongside the Police Act, 1861

1.4  Title: Bengal Jail Code / Bengal Prison Rules
     Year: 1894 (original); revised 1927, 1937
     Type: Rules
     Parent Act: Prisons Act, 1894 (Act IX of 1894)
     Subject: Prison administration, prisoner classification, discipline,
              diet, medical treatment, labor, visiting rules
     Key Contents:
       - Part I: General administration
       - Part II: Admission and classification of prisoners
       - Part III: Discipline and punishment
       - Part IV: Diet and clothing
       - Part V: Medical rules
       - Part VI: Under-trial prisoners
       - Part VII: Convict labor
       - Part VIII: Release and discharge
     Status: Active in Bangladesh (with amendments); forms the basis of
             current prison administration rules

1.5  Title: Rules under the Bengal Children Act, 1922
     Year: 1922 onwards
     Type: Rules
     Parent Act: Bengal Children Act, 1922 (Bengal Act II of 1922)
     Subject: Juvenile justice, reformatory schools, treatment of
              child offenders, neglected children
     Status: Replaced by Children Act, 1974 in Bangladesh; historical
             significance for SD-1 codification

1.6  Title: Rules under the Suppression of Immoral Traffic Act, 1933
     Year: 1933 onwards
     Type: Rules
     Parent Act: Bengal Suppression of Immoral Traffic Act, 1933
     Subject: Regulation of brothels, trafficking prevention
     Status: Amended/replaced post-independence

1.7  Title: Rules under the Arms Act, 1878 / Arms Act, 1959
     Year: 1878 onwards; Arms Rules 1924
     Type: Rules
     Parent Act: Indian Arms Act, 1878 (Act XI of 1878)
     Subject: Licensing of firearms, possession, manufacture, sale,
              import/export of arms and ammunition
     Key Sub-instruments:
       (a) Indian Arms Rules, 1924
       (b) Bengal Arms Rules (provincial supplement)
     Status: Active (as adapted); Arms Act replaced in Bangladesh by
             Arms Act, 1878 as adapted and subsequently the Arms Act, 2024

1.8  Title: Rules under the Explosive Substances Act, 1908
     Year: 1908 onwards
     Type: Rules
     Parent Act: Explosive Substances Act, 1908 (Act VI of 1908)
     Subject: Regulation of explosive substances
     Sub-instrument: Indian Explosives Rules, 1940
     Status: Active (as adapted)

1.9  Title: Rules under the Poisons Act, 1919
     Year: 1919 onwards
     Type: Rules
     Parent Act: Poisons Act, 1919 (Act XII of 1919)
     Subject: Regulation of sale and possession of poisons
     Sub-instrument: Bengal Poison Rules, 1922
     Status: Active (as adapted)

1.10 Title: Rules under the Official Secrets Act, 1923
     Year: 1923 onwards
     Type: Rules / Notifications
     Parent Act: Official Secrets Act, 1923 (Act XIX of 1923)
     Subject: Protection of state secrets, prohibited places, espionage
     Status: Active in Bangladesh

1.11 Title: Rules under the Foreigners Act, 1946
     Year: 1946 onwards
     Type: Rules / Orders
     Parent Act: Foreigners Act, 1946 (Act XXXI of 1946)
     Subject: Entry, residence, movement of foreigners
     Sub-instruments:
       (a) Foreigners Order, 1948 (post-partition adaptation)
       (b) Registration of Foreigners Rules, 1939
     Status: Active in Bangladesh (as adapted)

1.12 Title: Whipping Act Rules
     Year: 1909 onwards
     Type: Rules
     Parent Act: Whipping Act, 1909 (Act IV of 1909)
     Subject: Corporal punishment procedures
     Status: Repealed in Bangladesh

CATEGORY 2: CIVIL LAW AND PROCEDURE -- RULES AND REGULATIONS
================================================================================

2.1  Title: Civil Rules and Orders (Bengal)
     Year: Original framework 1908 onwards; consolidated multiple times
     Type: Rules / Practice Directions
     Parent Act: Code of Civil Procedure, 1908 (Act V of 1908)
     Subject: Practice and procedure of civil courts in Bengal -- filing
              of suits, service of summons, execution of decrees, appeals,
              review, revision, court fees, interlocutory applications
     Key Contents:
       - Volume I: General Rules
       - Volume II: Forms and Precedents
       - Rules regarding Pleaders and Mukhtars
       - Rules for Small Cause Courts
     Status: Substantially active in Bangladesh; forms the foundation of
             civil court practice. The "Civil Rules and Orders" as
             applicable to the territory of Bangladesh continue to govern
             civil procedure alongside CPC 1908.

2.2  Title: Rules under the Indian Evidence Act, 1872
     Year: Various notifications from 1872
     Type: Rules / Notifications
     Parent Act: Indian Evidence Act, 1872 (Act I of 1872)
     Subject: Evidentiary procedures -- examination of witnesses, admission
              of documents, expert evidence rules
     Note: Limited subordinate legislation directly under the Evidence Act;
           most procedural aspects governed through CrPC/CPC rules and
           High Court circular orders
     Status: Evidence Act active in Bangladesh; related rules continue

2.3  Title: Rules under the Indian Limitation Act, 1908
     (Later: Limitation Act, 1908, Act IX of 1908)
     Year: 1908 onwards
     Type: Rules / Notifications
     Parent Act: Limitation Act, 1908
     Subject: Time limits for filing suits, appeals, and applications
     Sub-instruments: Notifications specifying computation methods,
                      extension conditions
     Status: Active in Bangladesh (as adapted)

2.4  Title: Rules under the Specific Relief Act, 1877
     Year: 1877 onwards
     Type: Rules
     Parent Act: Specific Relief Act, 1877 (Act I of 1877)
     Subject: Specific performance, injunctions, declaratory decrees
     Status: Active in Bangladesh (as adapted); Specific Relief Act 1877
             continues to apply

2.5  Title: Rules under the Indian Succession Act, 1925
     Year: 1925 onwards
     Type: Rules
     Parent Act: Indian Succession Act, 1925 (Act XXXIX of 1925)
     Subject: Succession, wills, probate, letters of administration
     Sub-instruments:
       (a) Probate and Administration Rules (Bengal)
       (b) Court Fees applicable to succession matters (notifications)
     Status: Active in Bangladesh (as adapted)

2.6  Title: Bengal Money-Lenders Rules
     Year: 1940 onwards
     Type: Rules
     Parent Act: Bengal Money-Lenders Act, 1940 (Bengal Act X of 1940)
     Subject: Regulation of money-lending, interest rates, licensing
     Status: Active in Bangladesh (as adapted)

2.7  Title: Rules under the Guardian and Wards Act, 1890
     Year: 1890 onwards
     Type: Rules
     Parent Act: Guardians and Wards Act, 1890 (Act VIII of 1890)
     Subject: Guardianship of minors, custody proceedings
     Status: Active in Bangladesh

2.8  Title: Rules under the Indian Trusts Act, 1882
     Year: 1882 onwards
     Type: Rules
     Parent Act: Indian Trusts Act, 1882 (Act II of 1882)
     Subject: Trust administration
     Status: Active in Bangladesh (as adapted)

2.9  Title: Rules under the Indian Contract Act, 1872
     Year: Various notifications
     Type: Notifications
     Parent Act: Indian Contract Act, 1872 (Act IX of 1872)
     Subject: Declarations and notifications under the Act
     Status: Contract Act active in Bangladesh

2.10 Title: Bengal Court Fees Rules
     Year: Various dates; consolidated periodically
     Type: Rules
     Parent Act: Court Fees Act, 1870 (Act VII of 1870)
     Subject: Valuation of suits, computation and payment of court fees
     Status: Active in Bangladesh (with amendments)

2.11 Title: Rules under the Suits Valuation Act, 1887
     Year: 1887 onwards
     Type: Rules / Notifications
     Parent Act: Suits Valuation Act, 1887 (Act VII of 1887)
     Subject: Valuation of suits for jurisdictional purposes
     Status: Active in Bangladesh

2.12 Title: Insolvency Rules (Bengal)
     Year: Various; major rules under Presidency-towns and Provincial Acts
     Type: Rules
     Parent Act: Provincial Insolvency Act, 1920 (Act V of 1920);
                 Presidency-towns Insolvency Act, 1909 (Act III of 1909)
     Subject: Insolvency proceedings, adjudication, administration of
              insolvent estates
     Status: Active in Bangladesh (as adapted); Provincial Insolvency Act
             still applicable

CATEGORY 3: LAND REVENUE AND TENANCY -- RULES AND REGULATIONS
================================================================================

3.1  Title: Bengal Tenancy Rules
     Year: 1886 onwards; amended multiple times through 1938
     Type: Rules
     Parent Act: Bengal Tenancy Act, 1885 (Bengal Act VIII of 1885)
     Subject: Tenancy rights, rent fixation, transfer of holdings,
              survey and record-of-rights, arrear rent recovery,
              tenant-landlord relations
     Key Sub-instruments:
       (a) Rules for preparation of Record of Rights under BTA
       (b) Rules for commutation of rents
       (c) Rules for transfer of holdings
       (d) Rules relating to distraint for arrears of rent
     Status: Bengal Tenancy Act repealed in East Pakistan by the State
             Acquisition and Tenancy Act, 1950; however, many procedural
             rules adapted under the new Act. Historical significance for
             SD-1 codification. Rules under SAT Act 1950 are post-1947.

3.2  Title: Bengal Land Revenue Sales Rules
     Year: 1869 onwards
     Type: Rules
     Parent Act: Bengal Land Revenue Sales Act, 1868 (Bengal Act VII of 1868)
     Subject: Sale of land for arrears of land revenue, procedures for
              auction, setting aside of sales, annulment
     Status: Historically significant; superseded in many aspects by
             post-independence legislation but elements survive in
             adapted form

3.3  Title: Bengal Survey and Settlement Rules
     Year: 1875 onwards; major revisions 1898, 1908, 1935
     Type: Rules
     Parent Act: Bengal Survey Act, 1875 (Bengal Act V of 1875);
                 Bengal Tenancy Act, 1885
     Subject: Cadastral survey, settlement of rents, preparation of
              record-of-rights (khatian), map preparation, demarcation
     Key Sub-instruments:
       (a) Bengal Survey Rules, 1910
       (b) Rules for the Preparation of Record of Rights
       (c) Settlement Officers' procedural rules
       (d) Rules for Revisional Survey
     Status: Survey methodology rules still substantially influential;
             many adapted under the State Acquisition and Tenancy Act, 1950
             and the Survey Act framework in Bangladesh

3.4  Title: Rules under the Bengal Alluvion and Diluvion Regulation, 1825
     (Regulation XI of 1825)
     Year: 1825 onwards; rules updated through British period
     Type: Rules
     Parent Act: Bengal Alluvion and Diluvion Regulation, 1825
     Subject: Rights over newly formed land (char lands) in rivers,
              alluvion and diluvion rules
     Status: Active in Bangladesh; extremely relevant for char land
             disputes in riverine Bangladesh

3.5  Title: Rules under the Bengal Cess Act, 1880
     Year: 1880 onwards
     Type: Rules
     Parent Act: Bengal Cess Act, 1880 (Bengal Act IX of 1880)
     Subject: Local cess/tax on land for road and public works maintenance
     Status: Largely superseded; historical significance

3.6  Title: Bengal Land Registration Rules
     Year: Various; under the Bengal Permanent Settlement Regulation, 1793
           and subsequent revenue legislation
     Type: Rules / Regulations
     Parent Act: Permanent Settlement Regulation, 1793 (Regulation I of 1793);
                 Bengal Rent Act, 1859
     Subject: Registration of landholding, pattas, transfer procedures
     Status: Historical; superseded by post-independence land legislation

3.7  Title: Rules under the Bengal Land Revenue Commission
     Year: 1940 (Bengal Land Revenue Commission -- Floud Commission)
     Type: Rules / Notifications
     Subject: Land revenue assessment, commission procedures
     Status: Historical -- led to State Acquisition and Tenancy Act, 1950

3.8  Title: Rules under the Court of Wards Act, 1879
     Year: 1879 onwards
     Type: Rules
     Parent Act: Court of Wards Act, 1879 (Bengal Act IX of 1879)
     Subject: Management of estates of minors, lunatics, and incapacitated
              landholders
     Status: Active in Bangladesh (as adapted)

3.9  Title: Bengal Estates Partition Rules
     Year: 1897 onwards
     Type: Rules
     Parent Act: Bengal Estates Partition Act, 1897 (Bengal Act IV of 1897)
     Subject: Partition of jointly held estates, procedure, costs
     Status: Active in Bangladesh (as adapted)

3.10 Title: Rules under the Bengal Public Demands Recovery Act, 1913
     Year: 1913 onwards
     Type: Rules
     Parent Act: Bengal Public Demands Recovery Act, 1913
                 (Bengal Act III of 1913)
     Subject: Recovery of public demands (revenue, taxes, etc.) as
              arrears of land revenue -- certificate procedures
     Status: Active in Bangladesh as Public Demands Recovery Act, 1913
             (with amendments); rules continue to apply

3.11 Title: Rules under the Bengal Agricultural Debtors Act, 1936
     Year: 1936 onwards
     Type: Rules
     Parent Act: Bengal Agricultural Debtors Act, 1936 (Bengal Act VII of 1936)
     Subject: Relief of agricultural indebtedness, debt settlement boards
     Status: Historical; largely superseded

3.12 Title: Rules under the Non-Agricultural Tenancy Act, 1949
     Year: 1949 (pre-partition legislative process, enacted in East Bengal)
     Type: Rules
     Parent Act: East Bengal Non-Agricultural Tenancy Act, 1949
     Subject: Non-agricultural tenancy rights in urban/semi-urban areas
     Note: Technically East Bengal/Pakistan period but roots in pre-1947
           legislative process
     Status: Active in Bangladesh

CATEGORY 4: REGISTRATION AND STAMP -- RULES AND REGULATIONS
================================================================================

4.1  Title: Bengal Registration Rules
     Year: 1909 onwards; consolidated and amended multiple times
     Type: Rules
     Parent Act: Indian Registration Act, 1908 (Act XVI of 1908)
     Subject: Registration of documents -- compulsory and optional
              registration, procedure, fees, registration offices,
              indexes, copies, searches
     Key Sub-instruments:
       (a) Registration Rules (Bengal), 1909
       (b) Rules for maintenance of books and indexes
       (c) Rules for sub-registrar offices
       (d) Fee schedules (notifications)
     Status: Active in Bangladesh (as adapted); Registration Act, 1908
             continues to apply with Bengal Registration Rules as adapted

4.2  Title: Bengal Stamp Rules
     Year: 1899 onwards
     Type: Rules
     Parent Act: Indian Stamp Act, 1899 (Act II of 1899)
     Subject: Stamp duty rates, procedures for stamping instruments,
              adjudication, refund, penalty
     Key Sub-instruments:
       (a) Bengal Stamp Rules (under the Stamp Act, 1899)
       (b) Notifications specifying stamp duty rates for different
           categories of instruments
       (c) Rules for impressed and adhesive stamps
       (d) Stamping of court documents rules
     Status: Active in Bangladesh (as adapted); Stamp Act, 1899 continues
             to apply; rates revised by subsequent notifications

CATEGORY 5: MUNICIPAL AND LOCAL GOVERNMENT -- BY-LAWS AND RULES
================================================================================

5.1  Title: Bengal Municipal Rules / By-Laws
     Year: Various from 1884 onwards; major consolidation 1932
     Type: By-Laws / Rules
     Parent Act: Bengal Municipal Act, 1884 (Bengal Act III of 1884);
                 Bengal Municipal Act, 1932 (Bengal Act XV of 1932)
     Subject: Municipal administration, water supply, drainage, sanitation,
              building regulations, markets, roads, lighting, taxation
     Key Sub-instruments:
       (a) Bengal Municipal Building Rules
       (b) Bengal Municipal Water Supply Rules
       (c) Bengal Municipal Drainage and Sanitation By-Laws
       (d) Bengal Municipal Taxation Rules
       (e) Municipal Election Rules
       (f) Market Regulation By-Laws
       (g) Municipal Accounts Rules
     Status: Bengal Municipal Act 1932 was a key statute; replaced by
             various municipal ordinances and Acts post-independence but
             many by-laws continued in adapted form until formally
             replaced. Local Government (Municipality) Act 2009 now governs.
             Historical significance for SD-1.

5.2  Title: Calcutta Municipal By-Laws and Rules
     Year: Various from 1899 onwards
     Type: By-Laws / Rules
     Parent Act: Calcutta Municipal Act, 1899;
                 Calcutta Municipal Act, 1923 (Bengal Act III of 1923)
     Subject: Municipal governance of Calcutta
     Note: Less directly relevant to Bangladesh territory but influential
           as a model for Dhaka and Chittagong municipal by-laws
     Status: Not directly applicable to Bangladesh

5.3  Title: Dhaka Municipal By-Laws and Rules
     Year: Various from 1864 onwards (Dhaka Municipality established 1864)
     Type: By-Laws / Rules
     Parent Act: Bengal Municipal Act, 1884; Bengal Municipal Act, 1932
     Subject: Administration of Dhaka Municipality -- building, sanitation,
              water supply, roads, markets, taxation
     Status: Historical; superseded by subsequent legislation but
             foundational for Dhaka city governance

5.4  Title: Chittagong Municipal By-Laws
     Year: Various from 1863 onwards
     Type: By-Laws / Rules
     Parent Act: Bengal Municipal Act, 1884; Bengal Municipal Act, 1932
     Subject: Administration of Chittagong Municipality
     Status: Historical; superseded

5.5  Title: Bengal Village Self-Government Rules
     Year: 1919 onwards
     Type: Rules
     Parent Act: Bengal Village Self-Government Act, 1919
                 (Bengal Act V of 1919)
     Subject: Union boards, village courts, local governance at
              village/union level
     Key Sub-instruments:
       (a) Union Board Election Rules
       (b) Union Board Budget and Accounts Rules
       (c) Union Board Taxation Rules
       (d) Village Court Rules
     Status: Superseded by Basic Democracies Order 1959 and subsequently
             Local Government Acts in Bangladesh; historical significance

5.6  Title: Bengal Local Self-Government Rules
     Year: 1885 onwards
     Type: Rules
     Parent Act: Bengal Local Self-Government Act, 1885
                 (Bengal Act III of 1885)
     Subject: District and local boards -- establishment, powers,
              functions, finances
     Status: Historical; superseded by subsequent local government
             legislation

5.7  Title: Rules under the Bengal (Rural) Primary Education Act, 1930
     Year: 1930 onwards
     Type: Rules
     Parent Act: Bengal (Rural) Primary Education Act, 1930
                 (Bengal Act V of 1930)
     Subject: Compulsory primary education in rural Bengal,
              school management, teacher qualifications
     Status: Historical; superseded by post-independence education laws

5.8  Title: Rules under the Union Boards (Bengal Amendment) Act, 1943
     Year: 1943
     Type: Rules
     Parent Act: Bengal Village Self-Government (Amendment) Act, 1943
     Subject: Amendments to union board governance
     Status: Historical

5.9  Title: Bengal District Administration Rules
     Year: Various
     Type: Rules / Standing Orders
     Parent Act: Various enabling provisions; District Administration
                 conventions under Bengal Administration
     Subject: Revenue administration, magisterial functions, district
              officer duties
     Status: Many administrative practices continued post-independence

CATEGORY 6: EDUCATION -- REGULATIONS AND RULES
================================================================================

6.1  Title: University of Calcutta Regulations and By-Laws
     Year: 1857 onwards; major revisions 1904, 1920
     Type: Regulations / By-Laws
     Parent Act: Indian Universities Act, 1904;
                 Calcutta University Act, 1857 (Act II of 1857);
                 Calcutta University Act, 1904
     Subject: Degree requirements, examination rules, affiliation of
              colleges, academic governance
     Note: Calcutta University was the affiliating university for all
           colleges in Bengal including those in present-day Bangladesh
           territory until Dhaka University was established in 1921
     Status: Historical; but influenced the framework for Dhaka University

6.2  Title: University of Dhaka Regulations, Statutes, and Ordinances
     Year: 1921 onwards
     Type: Regulations / Statutes / Ordinances (university-level)
     Parent Act: Dacca University Act, 1920 (Act XIII of 1920)
     Subject: University governance, academic regulations, examination
              rules, affiliation, degrees, faculty appointments,
              student discipline
     Key Sub-instruments:
       (a) Statutes of Dhaka University (framed under the Act)
       (b) Ordinances of Dhaka University
       (c) Regulations for various degrees and examinations
       (d) Hostel regulations
       (e) Library regulations
     Status: Dhaka University Act 1920 was replaced by Dhaka University
             Order 1973; however, many regulations adapted and continued.
             Historical significance for SD-1.

6.3  Title: Indian Education Rules (Grant-in-Aid)
     Year: Various from 1859 onwards
     Type: Rules / Codes
     Parent Act: Various education resolutions and Acts
     Subject: Grant-in-aid to schools and colleges, conditions for grants,
              inspection, standards
     Key Sub-instruments:
       (a) Bengal Education Code (not a statute but an administrative code
           containing rules and regulations)
       (b) Grant-in-Aid Rules for primary schools
       (c) Grant-in-Aid Rules for secondary schools
       (d) Grant-in-Aid Rules for colleges
       (e) Rules for recognition of schools
     Status: Framework substantially continued; Bengal Education Code
             influenced post-independence education administration

6.4  Title: Bengal Primary Education Rules
     Year: 1919 onwards; revised 1930
     Type: Rules
     Parent Act: Bengal Primary Education Act, 1919;
                 Bengal (Rural) Primary Education Act, 1930
     Subject: Primary school management, teacher qualifications,
              curriculum standards, school construction, attendance
     Status: Historical; superseded

6.5  Title: Madrasah Education Rules and Regulations (Bengal)
     Year: Various from 1780s; reformed significantly 1870s, 1916, 1939
     Type: Rules / Regulations
     Parent Act: Administrative orders under Education Department, GoB;
                 Calcutta Madrasah Act
     Subject: Islamic education institutions -- curriculum, examinations,
              management, teacher qualifications, government grants
     Key Sub-instruments:
       (a) Bengal Madrasah Education Board Rules
       (b) Rules for Reformed Madrasah Scheme (1914 -- New Scheme)
       (c) Madrasah examination rules
     Status: Madrasah education system continues in Bangladesh;
             foundational rules influenced current framework.
             Bangladesh Madrasah Education Board now governs.

6.6  Title: Rules under the Bengal Private Universities Act
     Year: Not enacted during British period; but university affiliation
           rules under Calcutta/Dhaka University Acts served this purpose
     Type: Regulations
     Parent Act: Respective university Acts
     Subject: Affiliation and control of private colleges
     Status: Historical

7.9  Title: Rules under the Dangerous Drugs Act, 1930
     Year: 1930 onwards
     Type: Rules
     Parent Act: Dangerous Drugs Act, 1930 (Act II of 1930)
     Subject: Control of narcotic drugs -- opium, cocaine, hemp drugs
     Status: Superseded by Narcotics Control Act in Bangladesh

CATEGORY 8: TRADE, COMMERCE, AND INDUSTRY -- RULES AND REGULATIONS
================================================================================

8.1  Title: Bengal Factories Rules
     Year: 1911 onwards; revised under 1934 Act

      Subject: Auxiliary military police forces
      Status: Historical

      Year: 1904 onwards
      Type: Rules
      Parent Act: Ancient Monuments Preservation Act, 1904 (Act VII of 1904)

CATEGORY 19: WARTIME AND EMERGENCY REGULATIONS (1939-1947)
================================================================================

NOTE: During World War II, extensive emergency subordinate legislation was
enacted under the Defence of India Act, 1939 and Defence of India Rules.
Many of these were temporary but some provisions were continued post-war.

19.1 Title: Defence of India Rules, 1939
     Year: 1939 onwards
     Type: Rules
     Parent Act: Defence of India Act, 1939
     Subject: Comprehensive emergency powers -- detention, censorship,
              requisition, rationing, price control, movement restrictions
     Status: Expired; historical significance

19.2 Title: Bengal Food Grains Control Order
     Year: 1942 onwards
     Type: Order
     Parent Act: Defence of India Rules; Essential Supplies
                 (Temporary Powers) Act, 1946
     Subject: Food rationing, price control, procurement, distribution
              (enacted during Bengal Famine of 1943)
     Status: Historical; food control system evolved

19.3 Title: Bengal Foodstuffs (Control) Order
     Year: 1943 onwards
     Type: Order
     Parent Act: Defence of India Rules
     Subject: Control of foodstuff prices and distribution
     Status: Historical

19.4 Title: Bengal Cloth and Yarn Control Order
     Year: 1943 onwards
     Type: Order
     Parent Act: Defence of India Rules
     Subject: Textile rationing and distribution
     Status: Historical

19.5 Title: Essential Commodities Rules (Bengal)
     Year: 1946 onwards
     Type: Rules
     Parent Act: Essential Supplies (Temporary Powers) Act, 1946
     Subject: Control of essential commodities -- post-war continuation
              of wartime controls
     Status: Framework continued under Essential Commodities Acts

19.6 Title: Bengal Civil Defence Rules
     Year: 1939 onwards
     Type: Rules

     Status: Historical

CATEGORY 20: HIGH COURT RULES AND PRACTICE DIRECTIONS
================================================================================

NOTE: The High Court of Calcutta exercised jurisdiction over Bengal
(including present-day Bangladesh) until 1947. The Dhaka Bench of the
Calcutta High Court was established in 1905 (abolished 1911, re-established
at various points). After Partition (1947), the Dhaka High Court
(later the High Court Division of the Supreme Court of Bangladesh)
inherited many of the practice rules.

20.1 Title: Calcutta High Court Rules (Original Side)
     Year: Various from 1862 onwards
     Type: Rules
     Parent Act: Letters Patent, 1865 (Calcutta High Court);
                 Charter of the High Court
     Subject: Original jurisdiction proceedings, commercial suits,
              admiralty, testamentary, matrimonial jurisdiction
     Status: Historical; Dhaka High Court developed its own rules
             post-partition

20.2 Title: Calcutta High Court Rules (Appellate Side)
     Year: Various from 1862 onwards
     Type: Rules
     Parent Act: Letters Patent; CPC; CrPC
     Subject: Appeals, revision, reference procedures, criminal appeals,
              constitutional writs
     Status: Historical; foundational for High Court Division rules
             in Bangladesh

20.3 Title: Calcutta High Court Circular Orders
     Year: Various; compiled periodically
     Type: Circular Orders / Practice Directions
     Parent Act: Inherent jurisdiction of High Court; CPC/CrPC
     Subject: Court administration, case management, filing procedures,
              format of documents, adjournment policies, listing
     Status: Historical; Dhaka/Bangladesh High Court issued its own
             circular orders but inherited many practices

20.4 Title: Appellate Side Rules of the High Court (Bengal/Dhaka)
     Year: Various
     Type: Rules
     Subject: Filing of appeals, memoranda, cross-objections, stay
              applications, hearing procedures
     Status: Evolved into the current Appellate Division and High Court
             Division Rules of the Supreme Court of Bangladesh

IDENTIFIED IN THIS COMPILATION: Approximately 200+ individual instruments

KEY OBSERVATIONS:

1. MOST PROLIFIC SOURCES OF SUBORDINATE LEGISLATION:
   - Bengal Tenancy Act, 1885 (land and tenancy rules)
   - Code of Criminal Procedure, 1898 (criminal court rules)
   - Code of Civil Procedure, 1908 (civil court rules)
   - Indian Forest Act, 1927 (forest rules)
   - Police Act, 1861 (police regulations)
   - Bengal Municipal Act, 1932 (municipal by-laws)
   - Government of India Act, 1935 (service and administrative rules)
   - Defence of India Act, 1939 (wartime emergency rules)

2. STATUS DISTRIBUTION (ESTIMATED):
   - Active in Bangladesh (as adapted): ~35-40%
   - Amended but framework continues: ~20-25%
   - Superseded by post-independence legislation: ~25-30%
   - Repealed or obsolete: ~10-15%

3. CRITICAL DATA SOURCES FOR VERIFICATION:
   - Bangladesh Laws (bdlaws.minlaw.gov.bd) -- Ministry of Law digital repository
   - Bangladesh Gazette archives (Bangladesh Government Press, Tejgaon)
   - National Archives of Bangladesh, Dhaka
   - Gazette of India archives (National Archives of India, New Delhi)
   - Bengal Gazette / Calcutta Gazette historical archives
   - Supreme Court of Bangladesh Library
   - Dhaka University Law Library
   - Indian Kanoon (indiankanoon.org) -- for historical Indian legislation
   - CommonLII (commonlii.org) -- Commonwealth legal information
   - British Library India Office Records (for earliest period)

4. IMPORTANT METHODOLOGICAL NOTES:
   - Many instruments were published only in the Bengal Gazette and
     may not be available in digital form; physical archival research
     is essential for SD-1 (1799-1947 segment)
   - Post-Partition (1947) adaptations must be traced through the
     Pakistan Legal Framework Orders and East Bengal adaptation orders
   - Some instruments were made by the Central Government (GoI) but
     applied to Bengal Province specifically
   - University regulations, cantonment by-laws, and port trust rules
     constitute a significant body of quasi-legislative instruments
     that may or may not fall within the strict definition of
     "subordinate legislation" -- clarification from the Project Office
     may be needed
   - Wartime emergency legislation (1939-1945) generated an enormous
     volume of orders and notifications, many of which were temporary
     but some of which were continued through adaptation

5. KNOWN GAPS IN THIS COMPILATION:
   - Specific gazette notification numbers and dates require
     archival verification
   - Precise amendment histories for most instruments require
     gazette-by-gazette tracking
   - Some Bengal-specific by-laws of individual municipalities
     (beyond Dhaka and Chittagong) are not individually listed
   - Rules of individual educational institutions (beyond Dhaka
     University) are not comprehensively listed
   - Individual cantonment board by-laws are not separately enumerated
   - District-level administrative orders and notifications are
     not individually catalogued
