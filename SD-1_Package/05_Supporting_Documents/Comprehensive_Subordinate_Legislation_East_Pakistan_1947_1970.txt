ABBREVIATIONS AND NOTES
================================================================================

    C    = Central (Federal/Pakistan Government)
    P    = Provincial (East Bengal/East Pakistan Government)
    CMLA = Chief Martial Law Administrator
    MLO  = Martial Law Order
    MLR  = Martial Law Regulation
    SRO  = Statutory Regulatory Order
    GG   = Governor-General

    STATUS KEY:
    IN FORCE   = Still operative in Bangladesh (with or without amendments)
    SUPERSEDED = Replaced by subsequent Bangladesh legislation
    REPEALED   = Formally repealed
    HISTORICAL = Fulfilled its purpose; no longer operative
    OBSOLETE   = No longer applied though not formally repealed
    MODIFIED   = Core framework retained, substantially amended

    NOTE ON LEGAL CONTINUITY:
    Under Section 18 of the Indian Independence Act, 1947, all existing laws
    continued in force. Under Article 149 of the Constitution of Bangladesh
    (1972), and the Laws Continuance Enforcement Order, 1971 (P.O. No. 48 of
    1972), all Pakistan-era laws (including subordinate legislation) continued
    in force in Bangladesh subject to subsequent modifications or repeals.

A.1  ADAPTATION ORDERS (1947-1950)
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------

1.   Title: Pakistan (Provisional Constitution) Order, 1947
     Year: 1947
     Authority: Governor-General of Pakistan (C)
     Parent Instrument: Indian Independence Act, 1947 (Section 8)
     Description: Adopted the Government of India Act, 1935 as Pakistan's
         provisional constitution with adaptations. Established the federal and
         provincial government structure. Ensured legal continuity of all
         pre-partition laws, courts, and administrative machinery.
     Status: HISTORICAL -- Superseded by Constitution of 1956

     Subordinate instruments made under this Order:
     (a) Adaptation of Central Acts and Ordinances (various notifications)
     (b) Adaptation of Provincial Acts for East Bengal
     (c) Administrative orders establishing government departments in
         East Bengal/East Pakistan

2.   Title: Government of India (Adaptation of Existing Laws) Order, 1947
     Year: 1947
     Authority: Governor-General of Pakistan (C)
     Parent Instrument: Indian Independence Act, 1947
     Description: Systematic textual adaptation of all existing British Indian
         laws replacing: "British India" with "Pakistan"; "Governor-General of
         India" with "Governor-General of Pakistan"; "Indian Legislature" with
         "Pakistan Legislature"; and all provincial references adapted for
         Pakistan's provinces.
     Status: HISTORICAL -- Purpose fulfilled

3.   Title: General Clauses (Adaptation) Order, 1947
     Year: 1947
     Authority: Governor-General (C)
     Parent Instrument: Indian Independence Act, 1947
     Description: Adaptation of the General Clauses Act, 1897 for Pakistan,
         ensuring all defined terms in all existing legislation were properly
         adapted.
     Status: HISTORICAL

4.   Title: Pakistan (Adaptation of Central Acts and Ordinances) Order, 1949
     Year: 1949
     Authority: Governor-General (C)
     Parent Instrument: Provisional Constitution provisions
     Description: Further round of adaptations for central legislation; covered
         Acts and Ordinances not addressed in the 1947 order.
     Status: HISTORICAL

5.   Title: Pakistan (Adaptation of Existing Laws) Order, 1950
     Year: 1950
     Authority: Governor-General (C)
     Parent Instrument: Constitutional provisions
     Description: Comprehensive third-round adaptation of all existing laws to
         ensure uniform Pakistani terminology throughout all legislation.
     Status: HISTORICAL

6.   Title: East Bengal (Adaptation of Existing Laws) Order, 1950
     Year: 1950
     Authority: Governor of East Bengal (P)
     Parent Instrument: Government of India Act, 1935 (as adapted)
     Description: Provincial-level adaptation of inherited laws. Replaced
         "Bengal" or "Province of Bengal" with "East Bengal"; references to
         Calcutta High Court adapted for Dhaka High Court; administrative
         terminology updated for the new provincial administration.
     Status: HISTORICAL

7.   Title: East Bengal (Adaptation) Orders, 1948-1950 (Series)
     Year: 1948-1950
     Authority: Governor of East Bengal (P)
     Parent Instrument: Government of India Act, 1935 (as adapted)
     Description: Series of provincial adaptation orders addressing specific
         categories of inherited legislation. Individual notifications issued
         for: revenue laws, criminal laws, civil laws, educational legislation,
         municipal legislation, and service rules.
     Status: HISTORICAL

A.2  CONSTITUTION-RELATED ORDERS AND RULES
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------

8.   Title: Constitution (Adaptation of Laws) Order, 1956
     Year: 1956
     Authority: President of Pakistan (C)
     Parent Instrument: Constitution of Pakistan, 1956
     Description: Adaptation of all existing laws to conform with the first
         constitution of Pakistan. Changed references from provisional
         constitutional framework to the permanent constitution.
     Status: HISTORICAL -- Superseded when 1956 Constitution was abrogated

9.   Title: Constitution (Adaptation of Laws) Order, 1962
     Year: 1962
     Authority: President of Pakistan (C)
     Parent Instrument: Constitution of Pakistan, 1962
     Description: Adaptation of all laws to the second constitution (Ayub Khan
         era). Replaced references to parliamentary system with presidential
         system terminology; adapted National Assembly/Provincial Assembly
         references.
     Status: HISTORICAL -- Superseded when 1962 Constitution was abrogated

10.  Title: East Pakistan Provincial Assembly Rules of Procedure, 1956
     Year: 1956
     Authority: East Pakistan Provincial Assembly (P)
     Parent Instrument: Constitution of Pakistan, 1956
     Description: Rules governing the proceedings, debates, committee system,
         question hour, and legislative procedure of the East Pakistan
         Provincial Assembly under the 1956 Constitution.
     Status: SUPERSEDED

11.  Title: East Pakistan Provincial Assembly Rules of Procedure, 1963
     Year: 1963
     Authority: East Pakistan Provincial Assembly (P)
     Parent Instrument: Constitution of Pakistan, 1962
     Description: Revised procedural rules under the 1962 Constitution's
         presidential system. Governed legislative process including bill
         introduction, committee structure, budget procedure.
     Status: SUPERSEDED

12.  Title: East Pakistan Assembly Members' Privileges Rules, 1965
     Year: 1965
     Authority: Speaker, East Pakistan Provincial Assembly (P)
     Parent Instrument: East Pakistan Assembly Members' Privileges Act, 1965
     Description: Rules prescribing privileges and immunities of members of
         the East Pakistan Provincial Assembly, including freedom of speech,
         exemption from jury service, and procedural matters.
     Status: SUPERSEDED by Bangladesh Parliament framework

13.  Title: Election Rules, 1956 (Pakistan)
     Year: 1956
     Authority: Election Commission (C)
     Parent Instrument: Constitution / Electoral legislation
     Description: Procedures for voter registration, nomination of candidates,
         conduct of polls, counting of votes, election disputes.
     Status: SUPERSEDED

14.  Title: National Assembly and Provincial Assembly Electoral Rules, 1964
     Year: 1964
     Authority: Election Commission (C)
     Parent Instrument: Electoral College Act, 1964 / Constitution, 1962
     Description: Rules for indirect elections through Basic Democrats who
         formed the Electoral College for Presidential and Assembly elections
         under the 1962 Constitution.
     Status: OBSOLETE

15.  Title: Electoral Rolls Rules, 1965
     Year: 1965
     Authority: Election Commission (C)
     Parent Instrument: Electoral Rolls Act, 1964
     Description: Detailed rules for preparation, revision, and correction
         of electoral rolls.
     Status: SUPERSEDED

16.  Title: Representation of the People Rules, 1970
     Year: 1970
     Authority: Election Commission / President (C)
     Parent Instrument: Legal Framework Order, 1970
     Description: Rules for the 1970 general elections held on direct adult
         franchise (one man one vote) for the first time in Pakistan's history.
         Governed the election that gave the Awami League its landslide victory
         in East Pakistan (167 of 169 seats).
     Status: HISTORICAL significance

SECTION B: LAND, REVENUE, AND TENANCY RULES
================================================================================

1.   Title: East Bengal State Acquisition and Tenancy Rules, 1951
     Year: 1951
     Authority: Government of East Bengal (P)
     Parent Act: East Bengal State Acquisition and Tenancy Act, 1950
         (East Bengal Act XXVIII of 1951)
     Description: The most important subordinate land legislation. Prescribes
         procedures for: notification and acquisition of rent-receiving
         interests (zamindari abolition); preparation of Compensation
         Assessment Rolls (CA Rolls); assessment and payment of compensation;
         preparation of records of rights (khatians); revenue survey
         procedures; transfer of holdings and mutation; ceiling on holdings
         (100 bighas) and redistribution of surplus land.
     Status: IN FORCE in Bangladesh -- one of the most critical active rules

2.   Title: East Bengal State Acquisition and Tenancy (Amendment) Rules, 1955
     Year: 1955
     Authority: Government of East Pakistan (P)
     Parent Act: East Bengal State Acquisition and Tenancy Act, 1950
     Description: First round of amendments to acquisition procedures,
         compensation calculation methods, and record preparation.
     Status: MERGED into principal rules

3.   Title: East Bengal State Acquisition and Tenancy (Amendment) Rules, 1961
     Year: 1961
     Authority: Government of East Pakistan (P)
     Parent Act: East Bengal State Acquisition and Tenancy Act, 1950
     Description: Further procedural amendments including mutation form
         revisions and survey operation refinements.
     Status: MERGED into principal rules

4.   Title: East Pakistan Land Revenue Rules
     Year: 1950 (continuing from pre-partition Bengal Land Revenue Rules)
     Authority: Government of East Bengal (P)
     Parent Act: Bengal Land Revenue Sale Act, 1868 (Act VII of 1868) /
         Bengal Tenancy Act, 1885 (Act VIII of 1885)
     Description: Collection of land revenue, assessment of revenue demand,
         revenue default and sale procedures, remission of revenue in cases
         of crop failure or natural disaster.
     Status: PARTIALLY IN FORCE -- substantially modified

5.   Title: Non-Agricultural Tenancy Rules, 1949
     Year: 1949
     Authority: Government of East Bengal (P)
     Parent Act: Non-Agricultural Tenancy Act, 1949 (East Bengal Act XXIII
         of 1949)
     Description: Rights of non-agricultural tenants (urban and commercial
         land); rent assessment for non-agricultural tenancies; transfer and
         succession rules; eviction procedures and grounds; dispute resolution.
     Status: IN FORCE with amendments

6.   Title: East Pakistan Government Estates Manual, 1958
     Year: 1958
     Authority: Government of East Pakistan (P)
     Parent Act: Various land revenue statutes
     Description: Comprehensive manual for management of government estates
         (khas land), land settlement procedures, lease procedures, allotment
         of khas land to landless persons.
     Status: IN FORCE -- substantially amended

7.   Title: East Pakistan Land Reforms (Limitation of Land Holdings)
         Rules, 1961
     Year: 1961
     Authority: Government of East Pakistan (P)
     Parent Act: Land Reforms Ordinance, 1961 (Ordinance V of 1961) /
         Land Reforms Regulation, 1959 (MLR-64)
     Description: Implementation of ceiling on agricultural holdings
         (100 bighas/33.3 acres), procedures for surrender of excess land,
         redistribution to landless cultivators.
     Status: SUPERSEDED by subsequent Bangladesh legislation

8.   Title: East Pakistan Survey and Settlement Rules, 1962
     Year: 1962
     Authority: Government of East Pakistan (P)
     Parent Act: Survey Act, 1875 / Bengal Survey Act, 1875
     Description: Survey team composition and qualifications; cadastral
         and revisional survey methodology (plane table, chain survey); map
         preparation standards; record of rights (khatian) preparation;
         public verification (attestation) process; objection and correction
         procedure; final publication of records.
     Status: IN FORCE -- still governs Bangladesh land survey operations

9.   Title: Registration Rules, 1949 (East Bengal Adaptation)
     Year: 1949
     Authority: Government of East Bengal (P)
     Parent Act: Registration Act, 1908 (Act XVI of 1908)
     Description: Registration of documents, deeds, and property transfers;
         Sub-Registrar duties; registration fees; time limits; endorsement
         requirements; compulsory and optional registration.
     Status: IN FORCE with amendments

10.  Title: East Bengal Alluvion and Diluvion Regulations, 1952
     Year: 1952
     Authority: Government of East Bengal (P)
     Parent Act: General land revenue laws
     Description: Rules for dealing with newly formed chars (alluvion) and
         eroded land (diluvion) in riverine areas. Prescribes procedures for
         identifying new land, settlement, and compensation for lost land.
     Status: IN FORCE -- critical for Bangladesh riverine land issues

11.  Title: Chittagong Hill Tracts Land Revenue Regulation Rules
     Year: 1950 (adapted)
     Authority: Government of East Bengal (P)
     Parent Act: Chittagong Hill Tracts Regulation, 1900 (Regulation I of 1900)
     Description: Land revenue, settlement, and administration specific to
         the Chittagong Hill Tracts. Special provisions for indigenous
         communities.
     Status: IN FORCE with modifications under CHT Accord framework

12.  Title: Court of Wards Rules, 1951 (East Bengal)
     Year: 1951
     Authority: Government of East Bengal (P)
     Parent Act: Court of Wards Act, 1879 (Act IX of 1879)
     Description: Management of estates of disqualified proprietors, minors,
         and persons of unsound mind by the Court of Wards.
     Status: LIMITED applicability

13.  Title: East Pakistan Acquisition of Waste Land Rules, 1951
     Year: 1951
     Authority: Government of East Bengal (P)
     Parent Act: East Bengal (Emergency) Requisition of Property Act, 1948
     Description: Acquisition and distribution of waste and abandoned lands
         for productive use.
     Status: PARTIALLY IN FORCE

14.  Title: Land Acquisition Rules, 1963 (East Pakistan)
     Year: 1963
     Authority: Government of East Pakistan (P)
     Parent Act: Land Acquisition Act, 1894 (Act I of 1894)
     Description: Detailed procedures for compulsory acquisition of private
         land for public purposes. Covers notification, objection, award of
         compensation, market value determination, solatium, reference to
         court.
     Status: IN FORCE -- Land Acquisition Act, 1894 remained operative
         in Bangladesh until 2017 Amendment Act

15.  Title: East Pakistan Disturbed Persons Rehabilitation (Land Settlement)
         Rules, 1964
     Year: 1964
     Authority: Government of East Pakistan (P)
     Parent Act: East Pakistan Disturbed Persons Rehabilitation Ordinance, 1964
     Description: Settlement of land to persons displaced during communal
         tensions; refugee rehabilitation procedures.
     Status: OBSOLETE

SECTION C: FAMILY AND PERSONAL LAW RULES
================================================================================

1.   Title: Muslim Family Laws Rules, 1961
     Year: 1961
     Authority: Government of Pakistan (C)
     Parent Act: Muslim Family Laws Ordinance, 1961 (Ordinance VIII of 1961)
     Description: Comprehensive rules governing:
         Chapter I -- Marriage Registration: Appointment and qualifications
             of Nikah Registrars (Marriage Registrars); jurisdiction; marriage
             registration procedure; prescribed Nikahnama (marriage contract)
             form and contents; dower (mehr) recording; fee schedule; register
             maintenance.
         Chapter II -- Polygamy: Application for permission to contract
             another marriage; Arbitration Council procedure; conditions for
             granting permission; existing wives' consent/objection procedure;
             penalties for violation.
         Chapter III -- Talaq (Divorce): Prescribed notice form and procedure;
             service of notice on Chairman (Union Council); copy to wife;
             90-day waiting period; Arbitration Council formation for
             reconciliation; effect of non-reconciliation.
         Chapter IV -- Maintenance: Application for maintenance by wife;
             Arbitration Council procedure; maintenance order and enforcement;
             maintenance during iddat period.
         Chapter V -- Succession: Inheritance rights of grandchildren
             (orphaned grandchildren's share); procedure for claiming
             inheritance.
         Chapter VI -- General: Forms, fee schedule, penalties.
     Status: IN FORCE in Bangladesh -- critically important; applied daily by
         Union Council/Pourashava Chairmen, Nikah Registrars, and family courts

2.   Title: Muslim Marriages and Divorces (Registration) Rules, 1963
     Year: 1963
     Authority: Government of East Pakistan (P)
     Parent Act: Muslim Family Laws Ordinance, 1961 / precursor to Muslim
         Marriages and Divorces (Registration) Act, 1974
     Description: Nikah registration procedures; Nikah Registrar (Kazi)
         appointment, licensing, and jurisdiction; forms (Kabin-nama/
         Nikahnama); registration fees; maintenance of registers; annual
         returns to government.
     Status: IN FORCE -- directly governs marriage registration in Bangladesh

3.   Title: Family Courts Rules, 1965
     Year: 1965
     Authority: Government of East Pakistan (P)
     Parent Act: West Pakistan Family Courts Act, 1964 (adapted for
         East Pakistan)
     Description: Family court procedures for: dissolution of marriage;
         dower recovery; maintenance; custody of children; restitution of
         conjugal rights.
     Status: Framework continued under Family Courts Ordinance, 1985

4.   Title: Muslim Personal Law (Shariat) Application Rules, 1948
     Year: 1948
     Authority: Government of Pakistan (C)
     Parent Act: Muslim Personal Law (Shariat) Application Act, 1937
         (Act XXVI of 1937)
     Description: Application of Islamic personal law (Shariat) in matters
         of succession, inheritance, special property of females, wills,
         gifts, trusts, and religious endowments.
     Status: IN FORCE

5.   Title: Dissolution of Muslim Marriages Rules, 1948
     Year: 1948
     Authority: Government of Pakistan (C)
     Parent Act: Dissolution of Muslim Marriages Act, 1939 (Act VIII of 1939)
     Description: Procedures and grounds for judicial dissolution (faskh) of
         Muslim marriages -- cruelty, desertion, failure to maintain, impotence,
         insanity, etc.
     Status: IN FORCE

6.   Title: Child Marriage Restraint Rules, 1950 (East Bengal)
     Year: 1950
     Authority: Government of East Bengal (P)
     Parent Act: Child Marriage Restraint Act, 1929 (Act XIX of 1929)
     Description: Prevention of child marriages; age verification; penalties
         for solemnizing, abetting, or contracting child marriages.
     Status: SUPERSEDED by Child Marriage Restraint Act, 2017

7.   Title: Guardians and Wards Rules, 1950 (East Bengal)
     Year: 1950
     Authority: Government of East Bengal (P)
     Parent Act: Guardians and Wards Act, 1890 (Act VIII of 1890)
     Description: Procedures for appointment of guardians; custody
         proceedings; welfare principle; guardian's duties and powers;
         removal of guardian.
     Status: IN FORCE

8.   Title: Hindu Married Women's Separate Property Rules
     Year: 1948 (adapted)
     Authority: Government of East Bengal (P)
     Parent Act: Hindu Married Women's Right to Separate Residence and
         Maintenance Act, 1946 (Bengal Act)
     Description: Rules governing Hindu women's right to separate residence,
         maintenance, and stridhan (women's property).
     Status: IN FORCE

LEGAL CONTINUITY NOTE
================================================================================

Under Article 149 of the Constitution of the People's Republic of Bangladesh
(1972), and by virtue of:
- Laws Continuance Enforcement Order, 1971
- Bangladesh (Adaptation of Existing Laws) Order, 1972 (P.O. No. 48 of 1972)
- Bangladesh Laws Revision and Declaration Act, 1973 (Act VIII of 1973)

All Pakistan-era subordinate legislations continued in force in Bangladesh
subject to necessary modifications (replacing "Pakistan" with "Bangladesh,"
"East Pakistan/East Bengal" with "Bangladesh," "Governor" with "President,"
etc.) and specific repeals or amendments made from time to time.

Of the 250+ instruments identified in this compilation, approximately 127
remain in force in Bangladesh in whole or in substantial part, making this
the most practically significant body of subordinate legislation in the
SD-1 Code.
