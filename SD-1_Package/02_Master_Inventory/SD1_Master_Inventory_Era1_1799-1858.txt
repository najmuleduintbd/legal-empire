SECTION A: BENGAL REGULATIONS (1799-1834)
================================================================================

The Bengal Regulations were enacted by the Governor-General in Council and
constituted the primary body of codified law for the Bengal Presidency. While
quasi-legislative in nature, they functioned as delegated legislation under
the authority granted by successive Charter Acts.
